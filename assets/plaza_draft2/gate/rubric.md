# World gate drafts: rubric (written before the B revision was built or rendered)

Image-only, each criterion 0 to 10, mean rounded to 0.1. Bar: 7.0. A criterion under 5 fails the concept whatever the mean.
Judge both states; a criterion takes the worse state's score.

1. Distance: from the spawn camera (12 studs behind a 5.5-stud player) the gate reads as a gate, its state (sealed / open)
   and its colour are readable in the part the Index board and the sky-loop leg do not cover.
2. State: locked reads as a sealed gate (not a wheel, vault or wall), open as a way through; the change is obvious and has
   mechanical logic (hinges, travel, where the leaves go).
3. Focus: one dominant element per state, few competing ones; nothing looks like debris or noise.
4. Scale: believable beside a 5.5-stud player; doors, lock and posts sized for a landmark.
5. All sides: front, 3/4, back and from below have built structure, no flat or empty large face.
6. Fits the Rift Shrine: shares the plaza's timber, tile and stone, and carries the rift (colour, broken-space motif).
7. Original (design doc 5): no show's gate, portal, spiral or energy-ball motif, no text, logo or copied art.

Independent baseline before this revision (reviewer, 2026-11-25): A 5.8, B 7.5, C 6.8, current 4.0.
