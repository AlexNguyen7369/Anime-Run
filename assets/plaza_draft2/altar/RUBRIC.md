# Rubric, written before the first model (2026-11-25). Each line 0-10; pass is 8 on every line.

1. Extravagance: reads as the plaza's grandest service building in the 3/4 and front views; far bigger than 32 studs.
2. Spawn read: at player height from the spawn, the shape and the featured model's place are found in under a second,
   past the Saitamo pedestal and over the sky loop.
3. Focal point: the eye lands on the Mythic first; it is framed, lit and not lost against what is behind it.
4. Close-up craft: no floating scrap, gaps, z-fighting, see-through backs or bare slabs at player distance.
5. Locked state: clearly the same altar asleep; the silhouette of the Mythic still shows; not mistaken for unlocked.
6. Fit: Sakura dojo palette (wood, burgundy, cream, pink, gold, selective cyan), hard facets, original, no text or marks.
7. Clearance: no contact with a neighbour, the sky loop or its runners; the floor edge; the approach stays walkable.
8. Phone cost: inside the budget in RESEARCH.md.
9. Distinct: the three silhouettes cannot be confused in black.
