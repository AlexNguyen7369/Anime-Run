# Boss gate drafts: rubric (written before the gate was built or rendered)

Image-only, each criterion 0 to 10, mean rounded to 0.1. Bar 7.0; a criterion under 5 fails the draft whatever the mean.
A criterion takes the worse of its two states and, where both exist, the worse of the two worlds.

1. Distance: from the spawn camera it reads as a gate, and closed or open is readable.
2. State: closed is dark, shut and chained; open is lit and a way through; the change is obvious and mechanical.
3. Not the world gate: colour and silhouette differ from gate B (tiled gatehouse, violet or cyan) at a glance, and from the torii.
4. Identity: still draft A, a hewn stone ring between two spires with floating caps.
5. Support: ring and spires visibly stand on something; no part floats without a reason.
6. All sides: spire outer faces, back and 3/4 have built structure; no blank large face.
7. Contrast: stone separates from mountains, trees and sky in World 1 gold and World 2 dusk.
8. Light: no glow past the stones; the glow is one focus; no spiral, no energy ball.
9. Placement: the recommended spot is seen on arrival, clear of neighbours, and does not take over RACE START.
10. Original (design doc 5): no show's gate or emblem, no text, no logo.
