What each part of the scroll does

A real kakejiku has fixed parts. Each draft gives every part one job, so a player learns one object on both pages.

  1. Cord and hook. The scroll hangs from the dojo beam. On open it drops in, then unrolls (0.12 s drop, 0.5 s unroll, text inks in at 0.5 s).
  2. Ten (top mount) and futai strips. Brocade only, no text. It holds the page's colour.
  3. Ichimonji, the gold rule. It marks where reading starts and stops.
  4. Honshi, the paper. Everything you read: title, banner, featured unit, odds, pity, the team list.
  5. Chi (bottom mount). Where an action can sit, for the drafts that keep buttons on the scroll.
  6. Jiku, the roller. On close it rolls back up, in reverse, at 0.3 s.

Built in Roblox with 4 sliced images per skin (mount, gold rule, rod, roller cap) plus native text. Wordmark titles in Kaushan Script or Shippori Mincho are two baked images (Summon, Team); every other word uses a font Roblox ships.
