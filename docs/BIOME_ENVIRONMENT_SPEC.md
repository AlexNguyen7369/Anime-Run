# Biome environments: full-world design spec (goal `biomes`, b0)

Relies on `docs/GAME_DESIGN_REQUIREMENTS.md` sections 4.9 (biomes), 4.10 (world) and 5 (IP safeguards),
`docs/TRACKS_AND_BIOMES_SPEC.md` (Biomes, owner rulings, References) and `docs/WORLD_REDESIGN_SPEC.md` section 3
(spiral, plaza at origin, world about +-4150 studs). Owner direction 2026-10-30: rebuild all 10 track biomes as full
worlds, end to end: shaped Terrain + water, in-house 3D meshes, per-zone client atmosphere, ambient audio. Track
layouts (w3) and gameplay do not change. Numbers marked (placeholder) or (proposed) are tunable; the rest is decided.

Owner answers 2026-10-30 (decided, written in):
1. Terrain is generated by code at every server start: deterministic, in chunks, plaza first.
2. Agents generate and upload meshes, textures and sounds freely under the owner's account. Every asset id is
   recorded in a data module (section 4.4), and the b2 final summary lists them all.
3. Performance is PC-first with heavier effects. The heavy layers (particles, post-FX, far landmark detail,
   shadows) hook into the existing in-game REDUCED EFFECTS setting (section 8). No separate mobile budget.
4. Build and show in the Anime Run Team Create place (a collaborator is present).

## 1. Layers (what "full world" means)

| Layer | Owner | Built by | Replicates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terrain + water, shaped per biome | server | `TerrainBuilder` from `TerrainShape` (pure) | yes (streamed) |
| Road, checkpoints, pads (w3) | server | `TrackBuilder`, unchanged | yes |
| Dressing props: rhythm, scatter, blocks (w4) | server | `BiomeDressing`, now snapped to terrain height | yes |
| Hero landmarks + mid props as uploaded meshes | server | `BiomeAssets` (mesh registry), part fallback | yes |
| Sky, Lighting, Atmosphere, grade, bloom, clouds, water look, particles | client | `BiomeEnvironment` client | never |
| Ambient loop, one-shots, reverb | client | `BiomeEnvironment` client | never |

The server's `Lighting` stays at the `LightingConfig` baseline plus `WeatherService` (existing rule). Every
per-biome sky and light change is written on the client only and never replicates.

## 2. World structure

### 2.1 Zones
- Zone 0 is the plaza: the `SpawnArea` and the 7 Dojos plus a margin of 64 studs (placeholder). It uses the
  `LightingConfig` baseline profile.
- Zone n (1-10) covers every point whose nearest track centre line is Track n. Each zone holds its own ring band of
  the spiral, half of each gap to the neighbouring laps (the gap is about 110 studs edge to edge, 155 centre to
  centre), and, for Track 10, all space outside it.
- A track is about 1.5 laps, so a zone can face itself across a gap. The nearest-centre-line rule covers that case.
- Track ids and biome keys stay as they are (owner 2026-10-30):

| Track | Id | Biome key | Display name |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Oval | Speedway | Leafwind Village |
| 2 | Hill | Forest | Tournament Wasteland |
| 3 | DragRace | Docks | Pirate Harbor Isle |
| 4 | Circuit | Suburb | Wisteria Night Mountain |
| 5 | Serpentine | PirateCoast | Neon Cursed City |
| 6 | City | City | Hero Academy City |
| 7 | Desert | Desert | Blossom Shrine Path |
| 8 | Snow | Snow | Sky Islands |
| 9 | Volcano | Volcano | Spirit Realm Desert |
| 10 | Space | Space | Green Alien Planet |

### 2.2 Cross-section of a gap (every biome)
Let `d` be the distance from the nearest road centre line and `W/2 = 20` the road's half-width:

| Band | `d` (studs) | Terrain |
|---|---|---|
| Corridor | 0 to `W/2 + 24` = 44 (placeholder) | flat, top at `GROUND_TOP` (-1); never above road Y - 1; 12 studs of air above the road |
| Shoulder | 44 to 60 (placeholder) | smooth rise from the corridor to the biome's ridge profile |
| Ridge | 60 to gap midline (about 77) | the biome's `Ridge` shape (hill, cliff, dune, water, cloud drop) |
| Outer (Track 10 only) | past 77 on the outside | the biome's `Far` shape out to the skirt, 900 studs past Track 10's outer edge (placeholder) |

- Elevated road (slopes up to Rise 54): biomes with `Embank = true` raise the corridor to road Y - 3 (placeholder)
  under the slope, so hills sit under hills. Supports end up buried, which is fine. City biomes (5, 6) keep
  `Embank = false`: overpasses stay on their supports.
- Clear areas get flat terrain at `GROUND_TOP`: the plaza, Dojos, checkpoint areas and WinPads, each footprint plus
  `BiomeDressing.CLEARANCE`. These are the same roots `BiomeDressing.Prune` already excludes.

### 2.3 Where zones meet
- The seam between zones n and m lies on the gap midline (equal distance). Heights blend linearly across a 32-stud
  band (placeholder): the weight is `w = clamp(0.5 + (d_m - d_n) / 32, 0, 1)` on each biome's own height, so the
  seam never steps.
- Materials dither across the same band. A hash of (x, z) picks either biome's material with probability `w`.
- Along the route a zone changes at the finish checkpoint, where track n meets track n+1. The client blend
  (section 5.3) covers that change.

### 2.4 Safety baseplate
- It keeps its XZ rule (covers tracks, plaza and Dojos plus the margin; `world_layout` still passes).
- Once the terrain is built it moves down to Y = -60 (placeholder), below the deepest terrain floor, and becomes
  `Transparency = 1`. It stays `CanCollide`.
- Its role becomes the last-resort floor under water and the cloud drops. Terrain is the visible ground.
- If the terrain build fails, the baseplate stays where it is now and stays visible.
- The track `Ground` tiles stay as the dressing placement reference. Once the terrain is built they become
  `Transparency = 1`, `CanCollide = false`, `CanQuery = false`, which stops z-fighting with the terrain.

## 3. Terrain

### 3.1 Generated at server start (decided)
- Generated by code at every server start, deterministic (owner 2026-10-30). Reasons:
  - Rojo cannot sync terrain.
  - The track layouts are code, so terrain made from the same code follows any layout change.
  - Team Create collaborators never fight over a saved terrain.
  - A test can rebuild it.
- No terrain is saved in the place. b1 clears any saved terrain inside the world box before building.
- Build order in `Main.server.luau`:
  1. Chain.
  2. `SpawnArea`, Workshops.
  3. `BiomeDressing.Prune`.
  4. `TerrainShape.Prepare(entries, clearRoots)` (pure grid).
  5. `BiomeDressing.SnapToTerrain(trackModels)`.
  6. Baseplate block.
  7. `task.spawn(TerrainBuilder.Build)`.

  The services start while the terrain writes.
- Chunk order: plaza and Track 1 first, then zones 2..10 outward. `ReplicatedStorage` attribute `TerrainProgress`
  (0-1) and `TerrainReady = true` at the end. The harness waits for `TerrainReady`.
- Chunks are 256 x 256 studs (placeholder) in XZ, written with `Terrain:WriteVoxels` at resolution 4, with one
  `task.wait()` per chunk. Water goes in the same call (material `Water`, occupancy 1 below the biome's water level).
- Vertical range: floor Y = -24 (placeholder) to the top of the highest ridge. Under the corridor the terrain is only
  6 voxels deep.
- Budget: the whole world written in at most 45 s on the Studio server (placeholder target). b1 measures it and
  logs `[TERRAIN] built in Ns, chunks=N`.
- If the build is slower:
  - Raise the chunk size, or use `FillBlock` for the flat corridor and floor slabs (one call per chunk) and
    `WriteVoxels` only for the shaped top layer.
  - The plaza is always done in the first 2 s.

### 3.2 `TerrainShape` (shared, pure, no instances)
- `Prepare(entries, clearRoots)` stamps every track's world waypoints into a distance grid of 8-stud cells
  (placeholder). It stores a `buffer` per cell: the nearest track, its distance, the second-nearest track and its
  distance, and the road Y at the nearest point.
- The stamp radius is 120 studs (placeholder). Cells farther out count as "far" from their nearest track, measured
  with a coarse pass.
- Cost: about 8k segments x 400 cells. Run it once with yields.
- `Zone(x, z)`, `Height(x, z)`, `Material(x, y, z)` and `WaterLevel(zone)` read the grid (bilinear between cells)
  and the biome's `Terrain` profile (section 3.3). Noise is `math.noise` with a per-biome seed. Same inputs give the
  same world.
- `BiomeDressing.SnapToTerrain` uses `Height`: each dressing model drops onto the lowest of 5 samples (centre and 4
  footprint corners), so it is never left floating. Corridor props do not move, because corridor height is 0.
- Owner 2026-10-31 (fw2): every footprint point of a dressing prop must lie in its own track's zone (nearest track,
  or inside its corridor). A `Landmark_` model must also overhang by no more than 6 studs (placeholder): no
  footprint corner may sit more than that below the snapped base. A prop that fails slides toward its road 4 studs per
  try, up to 24 studs (both placeholders). It must keep its road clearance and must not overlap another prop. A prop
  that no slide fits is deleted. Exception: hero landmarks (4.3) are never deleted below 2 per biome, and a hero pinned
  to a route spot (`At`) is never deleted (owner 2026-10-31). A hero that cannot fit stays put and is reported as an
  error for the builder to move.
- Owner 2026-10-31 (fw2): the ground under a pinned (`At`) hero is levelled to its base Y. Its footprint, grown by
  CLEARANCE (8 studs, placeholder), is a clear box, like a checkpoint. The hero is not snapped or slid.
- Embank (fw2, 2026-10-31): the corridor sits 3 studs below the lowest road Y of its own track within 24 studs
  (`EMBANK_REACH`, placeholder), so it stays under the road on ramps.
- `ZonePolyline(n)`: one point per 64 studs (placeholder) of track n's centre line. The server publishes it as
  attribute `BiomeZone_<n>` on `ReplicatedStorage`, a string `"x,z;x,z;..."` (about 18 KB in total).

### 3.3 Per-biome terrain profile (data in the biome module, section 9)
```
Terrain = {
	Ridge = { Shape = "Hills"|"Cliff"|"Dunes"|"Mesa"|"Water"|"Drop"|"Blocks", Height = 30, Noise = 0.04, Amp = 8 },
	Far = { Shape = ..., Height = ..., Water = true? },          -- Track 10 only (outer skirt)
	Water = { Level = -3, Where = "Ridge"|"Far"|nil },           -- terrain water in ridge basins or the outer sea
	Materials = { Corridor = M.Ground, Ridge = M.Grass, Cliff = M.Rock, Bed = M.Sand },
	CliffSlope = 0.9,                                            -- slope (rise/run) above which Cliff material is used
	Embank = true,
	Craters = { Count = 12, Radius = 40, Depth = 8 },             -- optional; noise pits in the ridge band (placeholders)
}
```
- `Craters` (owner 2026-10-30): optional on any biome. `Count` round pits per track, placed deterministically in the
  ridge band outside the corridor, each a smooth bowl of radius `Radius` studs and `Depth` studs deep. Track 2 uses it.
- `Sections` (owner 2026-10-30): optional on any biome. `Sections = { { From = 0.2, To = 0.35, Side = "Inner"|"Outer"|"Both",
  Ridge = { ... }, Water = { Level = -3 }, Material = M.Cobblestone }, ... }`. `From` / `To` are fractions of the track
  length (0-1). Inside a section the given fields override the biome's `Ridge`, `Water` and corridor/ridge `Material`
  on that side; the edges blend over `SEAM` studs (placeholder). Fields left out keep the biome values. Track 1 uses it
  for the carved cliff, the river and the cobblestone square.
Shapes:
- `Hills`: cosine bump plus noise.
- `Cliff`: a near-vertical face, 70% of the way across the ridge band.
- `Dunes`: sine ripples along the road.
- `Mesa`: flat-topped steps.
- `Water`: the ridge dips to `Level - 6` and fills with water.
- `Drop`: the ridge falls to Y = -40 (the cloud deck, Track 8).
- `Blocks`: flat at 0. City blocks are the dressing `Blocks` layer.

## 4. Meshes

### 4.1 Pipeline
1. **Make**, one lane per asset:
   - Lane A (preferred): Studio `generate_mesh`, `generate_procedural_model`, `generate_texture` or
     `generate_material`, in Edit mode on the Anime Run studio (never Racer RNG).
   - Lane B: a Blender MCP model (low-poly, flat or 512 px colour textures), published from Studio as the owner's
     asset.
   - Lane C (small props only, no upload): the existing `BiomeMeshCodec` / EditableMesh path near the camera.
   - Lane D (owner 2026-10-30): a free Roblox Creator Store asset (mesh, model, texture, decal, sound, particle
     texture). Free only; no paid or limited items. Inserted in Edit mode, every Script / LocalScript /
     ModuleScript removed, then the meshes are read for their ids. Its ledger row records `Creator` and the
     Creator Store asset id. It passes the same IP review (step 3); character-shaped or fan-made anime assets are
     rejected.
2. **Prompt rule**: a generation prompt never names a show, a character, a real place or a brand. It uses generic
   words ("wooden village gate, red tiled roof, low poly anime style"). The prompt is stored in the ledger, and the
   real-name check scans it.
3. **IP review** before an id is used:
   - Go through the section 5 list and the track's References IP flag: no logos, crests, village or school symbols,
     flags, skulls, show kanji, real names, iconic exact silhouettes (the H-shaped tower, the named ship, the real
     carved faces, the film castle or robot).
   - A second agent (the rules auditor) checks a screenshot of the asset and signs `Reviewed = "<date> <who>"`.
   - Failures are regenerated, never edited to hide a symbol.
4. **Upload** under the owner's account (owner 2026-10-30) and record the id in the biome module's `Assets` ledger.
5. **Remove** the scratch instances from the place after recording the id. Nothing in the place is a source of
   truth.

### 4.2 Registry (`BiomeAssets`, server)
- When the dressing reads a prop def with `Mesh = "<AssetKey>"`, the registry loads the asset with
  `AssetService:CreateMeshPartAsync(Content.fromUri(id))` (pcall) on first use. It caches one template per key and
  clones it per placement. It applies `TextureContent` from the ledger, sizes the clone to the def's `Size` and
  anchors it. `CanCollide`, `CanTouch` and `CanQuery` are off.
- A load failure, or an unreviewed asset, builds the def's `Parts` instead. Every mesh def keeps a parts fallback, so
  the world is complete with zero assets.
- `RenderFidelity = Automatic`, `CollisionFidelity = Box`. `CastShadow` follows section 8.

### 4.3 Landmarks, mid props, horizon
- **Hero landmarks**: 2-4 per biome (section 7).
  - Tall rather than wide, to fit the gap: footprint at most 60 x 60 studs, height 60-220 studs (placeholders).
  - One model each, at points along the track where the route faces them (corner exits and straights 200+ studs
    long).
  - Optional `At = 0.8` on a hero def (owner 2026-10-30) pins it to that fraction of the track length, snapped to the
    nearest corner exit or 200+ stud straight. Hero defs without `At` keep cycling by index.
  - `ModelStreamingMode = Atomic`, `LevelOfDetail = SLIM` (falls back to `StreamingMesh` if SLIM is not available),
    so they stay visible past the streaming radius.
  - Up to 20k triangles each (the per-mesh import cap; placeholder until b1 checks it in Studio), one texture at
    1024 px.
- **Mid props**: 4-8 unique per biome. They replace the most-repeated multi-part w4 props (trees, houses, lanterns,
  rocks): one mesh instead of 3-12 parts. Up to 3k triangles each, one shared 512 px texture or none (built-in
  materials preferred, per Roblox's performance guidance).
- **Far backdrop**: a per-zone skybox (6 faces, 1024 px, `generate_texture`) painted with the biome's far silhouettes
  (the dome palace on the horizon, distant mountains, suns).
  - It costs no geometry, and it is the main tool for "visible from far" on the inner laps, where gaps are narrow.
  - The w4 `Horizon` layer stays only where the biome keeps it (section 7). Otherwise terrain ridges replace it.

### 4.4 Asset ledger (in each biome module)
```
Assets = {
	-- key = { Kind = "Mesh"|"Texture"|"Skybox"|"Sound", Id = "rbxassetid://N", Lane = "A"|"B"|"C"|"D",
	--         Prompt = "...", Creator = "<Lane D only>", Tris = N, Reviewed = "2026-10-30 auditor", Notes = "..." }
}
```
- Skybox rows (owner 2026-10-30): either one `Id` used on all 6 faces, or `Faces = { Bk, Dn, Ft, Lf, Rt, Up }`
  (6 ids) with `Id` set to the first face so every row has an `Id` for the checks.
- Only ids in a ledger are used. b2's final summary lists every ledger row (key, kind, id) for all 10 biomes
  (owner 2026-10-30).

## 5. Atmosphere (client only)

### 5.1 Zone detection
- `src/client/BiomeEnvironment.client.luau` reads the `BiomeZone_<n>` polylines once.
- Every 0.25 s (placeholder) it finds the nearest point to the camera focus (the runner while racing, the character
  otherwise). Zone 0 applies inside the plaza radius (attribute `PlazaRadius`, set by `TerrainShape`).
- A new zone must hold for 0.5 s (placeholder hysteresis) before the blend starts.
- The runner sits on its own track's centre line, so racing always gives the right zone.

### 5.2 Profile (data in the biome module; zone 0 = the `LightingConfig` baseline)
```
Atmosphere = {
	Sky = { Skybox = "<AssetKey>"|nil, SunTextureId?, MoonTextureId?, SunAngularSize = 21, MoonAngularSize = 11,
	        StarCount = 3000, CelestialBodiesShown = true },
	Lighting = { ClockTime, Brightness, Ambient, OutdoorAmbient, ExposureCompensation, ColorShift_Top,
	             EnvironmentDiffuseScale, EnvironmentSpecularScale },
	Atmosphere = { Density, Offset, Color, Decay, Glare, Haze },
	Grade = { Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, TintColor },      -- Lighting.Grade (ColorCorrectionEffect)
	Bloom = { Intensity, Size, Threshold },                       -- Lighting.Bloom
	SunRays = { Intensity, Spread },
	Clouds = { Cover, Density, Color },                           -- Terrain.Clouds
	Water = { Color, Transparency, Reflectance, WaveSize, WaveSpeed }, -- Terrain.Water* (client-local)
	Particles = { { Texture, Color, Size, Rate, Lifetime, Speed, SpreadAngle, LightEmission, Area = Vector3 } },
}
```
- The client writes the existing replicated instances `Lighting.Grade`, `Bloom`, `SunRays`, `Atmosphere`, the `Sky`
  and `Terrain.Clouds` locally. It creates no second ColorCorrection.
- Local writes stay until the server writes the same property again. Only `WeatherService` does that. The client
  listens with `GetPropertyChangedSignal` on the properties weather writes and re-applies its blended value on the
  next frame.
- Weather on top of the biome: while `ReplicatedStorage.WeatherEvent` is an event with a `Sky` (`WeatherConfig`), the
  final value is `lerp(biome, weatherTarget, 0.5)` (placeholder) for `ClockTime`, `Brightness`, `OutdoorAmbient`,
  Atmosphere and Clouds. A Night Race event darkens a day biome, and a night biome stays night.
- Particles: one client-only `Part` (`CanCollide`, `CanQuery` and `CanTouch` off, `Transparency = 1`), size `Area`,
  kept 20 studs (placeholder) ahead of the camera. It holds at most 2 emitters per profile, with Rate at most 40 per
  emitter (placeholder). Textures are in-house, made with the roblox-vfx method (numpy-drawn, uploaded, listed in the
  ledger).

### 5.3 Blend
- Every numeric and colour property lerps from its current value to the target over `BLEND_SECONDS = 3`
  (placeholder), with a sine ease, per frame.
- A Sky texture cannot lerp. At the blend midpoint Atmosphere `Haze` peaks at +1.5 and `Density` at +0.15
  (placeholder), and the Skybox ids swap under the haze.
- Particles fade their Rate out over the first half and in over the second.
- A new zone during a blend starts from the current mid-blend values.

## 6. Audio (client only)
- Source: in-house loops and one-shots. Agents make them with python3 (numpy filtered noise, tones, layered and
  looped seamlessly, 30-60 s, OGG) in `assets/audio/<BiomeKey>/` and upload them under the owner's account (owner
  2026-10-30). Ids go in the ledger. Free Creator Store sounds (Lane D, owner 2026-10-30) are allowed too, with
  the same ledger row (id + creator) and no official show audio.
- Per biome: 1 loop and at most 2 one-shots. One-shots play at random every 10-25 s (placeholder), at random
  positions 30-80 studs from the camera, volume 0.15-0.25.
- Loop volume is 0.3 (placeholder, per-biome override 0.2-0.45). The sounds live in a client-made `SoundGroup`
  "Ambience" (Volume 1) under `SoundService`.
- Two Sound objects A/B crossfade over `BLEND_SECONDS`, in step with the atmosphere blend.
- `SoundService.AmbientReverb` is set per zone (client-local) at the blend midpoint.
- The existing Settings SOUND toggle already mutes every Sound in the game (`RacerUiController` `applySound` on
  `DescendantAdded`). No new mute is needed.
- A missing id gives silence for that layer, never an error.

## 11. Research takeaways (general conventions only, nothing copied)
- Big anime-themed Roblox worlds are split into clearly themed zones (islands, seas, worlds). Each zone has its own
  silhouette and palette, and the player knows at a glance where they are.
- Zone mood changes on the client when the player enters, by tweening Atmosphere, ColorCorrection and Lighting
  values. Tween or lerp from current values, so reversing mid-transition stays smooth.
- Terrain carries ground and water cheaply. Meshes carry the landmarks. Built-in materials beat custom textures for
  memory, and reused meshes beat duplicates.
- Large places run with instance streaming. Model LOD (SLIM / StreamingMesh) keeps far content visible past the
  streaming radius.
- Budget draw calls and triangles against a baseline device (about 1,000 draw calls and 1M triangles); particle
  emitters each cost a draw call.
