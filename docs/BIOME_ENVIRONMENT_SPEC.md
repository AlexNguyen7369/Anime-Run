# Biome environments: full-world design spec

Relies on `docs/GAME_DESIGN_REQUIREMENTS.md` sections 4.9 (biomes), 4.10 (world) and 5 (IP safeguards),
`docs/TRACKS_AND_BIOMES_SPEC.md` (Biomes, owner rulings, References) and `docs/WORLD_REDESIGN_SPEC.md` section 3
(spiral, plaza at origin, world about +-4150 studs). Owner direction 2026-10-30: all 10 track biomes are full worlds,
end to end: shaped Terrain + water, in-house 3D meshes, per-zone client atmosphere, ambient audio. Numbers marked
(placeholder) or (proposed) are tunable; the rest is decided.

Owner answers 2026-10-30:
1. Terrain is generated by code at every server start: deterministic, in chunks, plaza first.
2. Agents generate and upload meshes, textures and sounds freely under the owner's account. Every asset id is
   recorded in a data module (section 4.4), and the biome final summary lists them all.
3. Performance is PC-first with heavier effects. The heavy layers (particles, post-FX, far landmark detail,
   shadows) hook into the existing in-game REDUCED EFFECTS setting (section 8). No separate mobile budget.
4. Build and show in the Anime Run Team Create place (a collaborator is present).

## 1. Layers (what "full world" means)

| Layer | Owner | Built by | Replicates |
|---|---|---|---|
| Terrain + water, shaped per biome | server | `TerrainBuilder` from `TerrainShape` (pure) | yes (streamed) |
| Road, checkpoints, pads | server | `TrackBuilder` | yes |
| Dressing props: rhythm, scatter, blocks | server | `BiomeDressing`, snapped to terrain height | yes |
| Hero landmarks + mid props as uploaded meshes | server | `BiomeAssets` (mesh registry), part fallback | yes |
| Sky, Lighting, Atmosphere, grade, bloom, clouds, water look, particles | client | `BiomeEnvironment` client | never |
| Ambient loop, one-shots, reverb | client | `BiomeEnvironment` client | never |

The server's `Lighting` stays at the `LightingConfig` baseline plus `WeatherService`. Every per-biome sky and light
change is written on the client only and never replicates.

## 2. World structure

### 2.1 Zones
- Zone 0 is the plaza: the `SpawnArea` and the 7 Dojos plus a margin of 64 studs (placeholder). It has its own
  client-local golden-hour profile (owner 2026-11-03, design doc 4.24; replaces the `LightingConfig` baseline here).
  The server's `Lighting` stays the `LightingConfig` baseline.
- Zone n (1-10) covers every point whose nearest track centre line is Track n. Each zone holds its own ring band of
  the spiral, half of each gap to the neighbouring laps (the gap is about 110 studs edge to edge, 155 centre to
  centre), and, for Track 10, all space outside it.
- A track is about 1.5 laps, so a zone can face itself across a gap. The nearest-centre-line rule covers that case.
- Track ids and biome keys stay as they are (owner 2026-10-30):

| 2 | Hill | Forest | Tournament Wasteland |
| 3 | DragRace | Docks | Pirate Harbor Isle |
| 4 | Circuit | Suburb | Wisteria Night Mountain |
| 5 | Serpentine | PirateCoast | Burning Cursed City |
| 6 | City | City | Hero Academy City |
| 7 | Desert | Desert | Shibuyo Underground |
| 8 | Snow | Snow | Skypeia |

### 2.2 Cross-section of a gap (every biome)
Let `d` be the distance from the nearest road centre line and `W/2 = 20` the road's half-width:

| Band | `d` (studs) | Terrain |
|---|---|---|
| Corridor | 0 to `W/2 + 24` = 44 (placeholder) | flat, top at `GROUND_TOP` (-1); never above road Y - 1; 12 studs of air above the road |
| Shoulder | 44 to 60 (placeholder) | smooth rise from the corridor to the biome's ridge profile |
| Ridge | 60 to gap midline (about 77) | the biome's `Ridge` shape (hill, cliff, dune, water, cloud drop) |
| Outer (Track 10 only) | past 77 on the outside | the biome's `Far` shape out to the skirt, 900 studs past Track 10's outer edge (placeholder) |

- Elevated road (slopes up to Rise 54): biomes with `Embank = true` raise the corridor to road Y - 3 (placeholder)
  under the slope, so hills sit under hills. Supports end up buried, which is fine. City biomes (5, 6) keep
  `Embank = false`: overpasses stay on their supports.
- Clear areas get flat terrain at `GROUND_TOP`: the plaza, Dojos, checkpoint areas and WinPads, each footprint plus
  `BiomeDressing.CLEARANCE`. These are the same roots `BiomeDressing.Prune` already excludes.

### 2.3 Where zones meet
- The seam between zones n and m lies on the gap midline (equal distance). Heights blend linearly across a 32-stud
  band (placeholder): the weight is `w = clamp(0.5 + (d_m - d_n) / 32, 0, 1)` on each biome's own height, so the
  seam never steps.
- Materials dither across the same band. A hash of (x, z) picks either biome's material with probability `w`.
- Along the route a zone changes at the finish checkpoint, where track n meets track n+1. The client blend
  (section 5.3) covers that change.

### 2.4 Safety baseplate
- It keeps its XZ rule (covers tracks, plaza and Dojos plus the margin; `world_layout` still passes).
- Once the terrain is built it moves down to Y = -60 (placeholder), below the deepest terrain floor, and becomes
  `Transparency = 1`. It stays `CanCollide`. It is the last-resort floor under water and the cloud drops.
- If the terrain build fails, the baseplate stays where it is and stays visible.
- The track `Ground` tiles stay as the dressing placement reference. Once the terrain is built they become
  `Transparency = 1`, `CanCollide = false`, `CanQuery = false`, which stops z-fighting with the terrain.

- Owner 2026-11-03: a track's finish pad may take its biome's palette colour (it need not be yellow). Track 6 keeps one
  street-wall block row.

## 3. Terrain

### 3.1 Generated at server start (decided)
- Generated by code at every server start, deterministic (owner 2026-10-30): Rojo cannot sync terrain, and terrain made
  from the track code follows any layout change.
- No terrain is saved in the place. The builder clears any saved terrain inside the world box first.
- Build order in `Main.server.luau`: Chain; `SpawnArea`, Workshops; `BiomeDressing.Prune`;
  `TerrainShape.Prepare(entries, clearRoots)` (pure grid); `BiomeDressing.SnapToTerrain(trackModels)`; baseplate
  block; `task.spawn(TerrainBuilder.Build)`. The services start while the terrain writes.
- Chunk order: plaza and Track 1 first, then zones 2..10 outward. `ReplicatedStorage` attribute `TerrainProgress`
  (0-1), `TerrainNearReady = true` once the plaza and Track 1 are written and their stand-in ground is cleared (the loading screen waits for this), and `TerrainReady = true` at the end. The harness waits for `TerrainReady`.
- Chunks are 256 x 256 studs (placeholder) in XZ, written with `Terrain:WriteVoxels` at resolution 4, with one
  `task.wait()` per chunk. Water goes in the same call (material `Water`, occupancy 1 below the biome's water level).
  Each chunk's shaped layer starts below both its lowest column top and any tunnel hollow floor in it, so the floor
  fill never closes a tunnel.
- Vertical range: floor Y = -24 (placeholder) to the top of the highest ridge. Under the corridor the terrain is only
  6 voxels deep.
- Budget: the whole world written in at most 45 s on the Studio server (placeholder target); logged as
  `[TERRAIN] built in Ns, chunks=N`.
- If the build is slower: raise the chunk size, or use `FillBlock` for the flat corridor and floor slabs (one call per
  chunk) and `WriteVoxels` only for the shaped top layer. The plaza is always done in the first 2 s.

### 3.2 `TerrainShape` (shared, pure, no instances)
- `Prepare(entries, clearRoots)` stamps every track's world waypoints into a distance grid of 8-stud cells
  (placeholder). It stores a `buffer` per cell: the nearest track, its distance, the second-nearest track and its
  distance, and the road Y at the nearest point.
- The stamp radius is 120 studs (placeholder). Cells farther out count as "far" from their nearest track, measured
  with a coarse pass. Run it once with yields.
- `Zone(x, z)`, `Height(x, z)`, `Material(x, y, z)` and `WaterLevel(zone)` read the grid (bilinear between cells)
  and the biome's `Terrain` profile (section 3.3). Noise is `math.noise` with a per-biome seed. Same inputs give the
  same world.
- `BiomeDressing.SnapToTerrain` uses `Height`: each dressing model drops onto the lowest of 5 samples (centre and 4
  footprint corners), so it is never left floating. Corridor props do not move, because corridor height is 0.
- Owner 2026-10-31: every footprint point of a dressing prop must lie in its own track's zone (nearest track,
  or inside its corridor). A `Landmark_` model must also overhang by no more than 6 studs (placeholder): no
  footprint corner may sit more than that below the snapped base. A prop that fails slides toward its road 4 studs per
  try, up to 24 studs (both placeholders). It must keep its road clearance and must not overlap another prop. A prop
  that no slide fits is deleted. Exception: hero landmarks (4.3) are never deleted below 2 per biome, and a hero pinned
  to a route spot (`At`) is never deleted (owner 2026-10-31). A hero that cannot fit stays put and is reported as an
  error for the builder to move.
- Owner 2026-10-31: the ground under a pinned (`At`) hero is levelled to the natural terrain height at its centre
  (`TerrainShape.AddPad`), and the hero is raised onto it; it is not slid (except the outward push below). The pad
  covers its footprint grown by CLEARANCE (8 studs, placeholder) and is flat at that height over every track's ridge and
  seam, also across the gap midline. Outside that it blends out over 16 studs (SHOULDER - CORRIDOR). Road and clear-box
  ground never change: the pad stops at any corridor edge, and a hero whose footprint reaches into a corridor is
  reported as an error. No other prop, of any track, may overlap the grown footprint: it slides or is deleted. A hero
  that would touch a road's clearance stays at its base.
- Owner 2026-10-31: house-like props (defs with `Pad = true`: every `Kit.house`) get a small levelled pad instead of the
  downhill float. It uses the same pad as a pinned hero, at the natural height at the prop's centre, covering its
  footprint plus `PAD_MARGIN` 4 studs (one voxel, placeholder, so every column next to the footprint is flat pad). Pads
  never touch corridors or clear boxes. Padded props snap first, so every other prop snaps onto ground that already has
  their pads. Pads are looked up through 64-stud buckets (placeholder).
- Pad details (values placeholders):
  - A pad is the prop's oriented footprint (its own yaw), not the world box around it.
  - Where flat areas meet, the first pad rules and no later pad's blend changes it. Pinned heroes pad first, then houses
    in snap order.
  - Every padded prop gets its pad, also when it does not move (centre already at corridor height).
  - A padded prop may not overlap another padded prop's pad (both footprints grown by `PAD_MARGIN`): it slides or is
    deleted.
  - A padded prop whose footprint is outside every corridor but whose pad reaches one is pushed outward, away from its
    road, SNAP_SLIDE_STEP (4) studs per try up to SNAP_SLIDE_MAX (24), to the first spot where its pad is clear of the
    corridor, it stays in its zone, and it overlaps no pad or prop. With no such spot it takes the corridor seat.
  - Corridor seat: a padded prop whose footprint plus `PAD_MARGIN` reaches a corridor or clear box sits on the lowest
    corridor ground under that grown footprint; its pad levels the rest to that height. Corridor ground never changes.
  - A pinned hero whose footprint reaches a corridor or clear box is pushed outward the same way, to the first spot
    where it is clear. If none is clear it stays put and is reported as an error.
  - `TerrainShape.CorridorLow(cf, size)` samples the oriented footprint every 4 studs, edges included.
  - Check: `lune run tools/lune/biome_harness.luau` (all tracks; reports gaps on `Height` and on the drawn voxel
    surface).
- Embank: the corridor sits 3 studs below the lowest road Y of its own track within 24 studs
  (`EMBANK_REACH`, placeholder), so it stays under the road on ramps.
- `ZonePolyline(n)`: one point per 64 studs (placeholder) of track n's centre line. The server publishes it as
  attribute `BiomeZone_<n>` on `ReplicatedStorage`, a string `"x,z;x,z;..."` (about 18 KB in total).

### 3.3 Per-biome terrain profile (data in the biome module, section 9)
```
Terrain = {
	Ridge = { Shape = "Hills"|"Cliff"|"Dunes"|"Mesa"|"Water"|"Drop"|"Blocks", Height = 30, Noise = 0.04, Amp = 8 },
	Far = { Shape = ..., Height = ..., Water = true? },          -- Track 10 only (outer skirt)
	Water = { Level = -3, Where = "Ridge"|"Far"|nil },           -- terrain water in ridge basins or the outer sea
	Materials = { Corridor = M.Ground, Ridge = M.Grass, Cliff = M.Rock, Bed = M.Sand },
	CliffSlope = 0.9,                                            -- slope (rise/run) above which Cliff material is used
	Embank = true,
	Craters = { Count = 12, Radius = 40, Depth = 8 },             -- optional; noise pits in the ridge band (placeholders)
}
```
- `Craters` (owner 2026-10-30): optional on any biome. `Count` round pits per track, placed deterministically in the
  ridge band outside the corridor, each a smooth bowl of radius `Radius` studs and `Depth` studs deep.
- `Sections` (owner 2026-10-30): optional on any biome. `Sections = { { From = 0.2, To = 0.35, Side = "Inner"|"Outer"|"Both",
  Ridge = { ... }, Water = { Level = -3 }, Material = M.Cobblestone }, ... }`. `From` / `To` are fractions of the track
  length (0-1). Inside a section the given fields override the biome's `Ridge`, `Water` and corridor/ridge `Material`
  on that side; the edges blend over `SEAM` studs (placeholder). Fields left out keep the biome values.

Shapes:
- `Hills`: cosine bump plus noise.
- `Cliff`: a near-vertical face, 70% of the way across the ridge band.
- `Dunes`: sine ripples along the road.
- `Mesa`: flat-topped steps.
- `Water`: the ridge dips to `Level - 6` and fills with water.
- `Drop`: the ridge falls to Y = -40 (the cloud deck, Track 8).
- `Blocks`: flat at 0. City blocks are the dressing `Blocks` layer.

### 3.5 Enclosed tunnel corridor (proposed)
Owner 2026-10-31: Track 7 runs inside a tunnel for its whole length. The biome module only supplies data; the framework
(`src/shared/Tunnel.luau`) builds it. All values are placeholders.
- **Profile field** (optional on any biome, used by Track 7 only):
  ```
  Tunnel = {
  	Width = 120, Height = 26,                      -- target inner width (narrowed per side, below) and height
  	Wall = { Material = Enum.Material.CeramicTiles, Color = "#E9E4D6", Stripe = "#3FA7A0" },
  	Ceiling = { Material = Enum.Material.Concrete, Color = "#8C8A84" },
  	Floor = { Material = Enum.Material.Concrete, Color = "#8C8A84" },
  	LightEvery = 16,                               -- one ceiling light strip every N studs
  	Bays = { { From = 0.12, To = 0.17, Side = "Left"|"Right"|"Both", Depth = 40, Height = 30 }, ... },
  	Portals = true,                                -- stair-entrance facades at the start and finish
  }
  ```
- **Builder**: a server step after `TrackBuilder` and before dressing. It walks the centre line and places anchored wall
  and ceiling pieces per segment (corners as short chords), following road Y on slopes. The ceiling sits at road Y +
  `Height`. Walls clear every checkpoint, pad and WinPad footprint by `CLEARANCE`. Inside a bay (`From` / `To` are
  fractions of track length, like `Sections`) the wall on `Side` steps out by `Depth` and the ceiling rises to the bay
  `Height`; the bay holds a raised platform floor and the dressing's platform props. Portals at both ends join the
  open-air Tracks 6 and 8.
- **Adaptive width** (owner 2026-11-01: very wide along the whole route, not cramped; values placeholders): `Width` is a
  target. `Tunnel.Profile` works out each side's inner half-width every `STEP` (8) studs. It is `min(Width / 2, room)`.
  `room` keeps the tunnel box (wall + `MARGIN`) `ROOM_MARGIN` (4) studs off every other track's corridor (`CORRIDOR`,
  44, from its centre line). It also keeps the box off this track's own far laps (more than `SELF_GAP`, 400 studs, along
  the road), each counted as a whole tunnel wide. The room changes by at most `TAPER` (0.25) studs per stud along the
  road, as the lower envelope of the raw room. The walls slope between width stations and never step, except at a bay
  end. The width never drops below road half-width + 2 + `CLEARANCE` (30 on Track 7). A station with less raw room is
  reported (a crossing to fix).
  - Bays: a bay platform's road-side edge is `PLATFORM_EDGE` (30). A bay wall stands at `PLATFORM_EDGE + Depth` where
    the room allows, never inside the main wall. Bay `Height` is unchanged.
  - Every station where a wall changes is a segment cut, so a segment's ends differ by at most `TAPER x STEP` (2) studs.
    Walls and stripes run straight between the ends' widths. Ceiling, floor and platforms span the wider end and
    reach at most 2 studs into the wall. Caps close only real steps (bay ends). Portals take the end widths.
  - The terrain box and the earth over the roof follow the segments; other tracks' corridors never change.
  - Dressing: at road level a tunnel prop stands between the road edge + `CLEARANCE` (Prune's road clearance) and
    the wall at the width there, so the wall-side strips along the whole route are prop space. Its wall gap also loses
    `CHORD_XZ` (the walls follow the chords) and samples the width every 4 studs along its length. An `Offset` past the
    widest wall puts a layer's props against the wall (Track 7 Rhythm).
  - Check: `TUNNEL=1 lune run tools/lune/biome_harness.luau` prints the per-side width (min / avg / max outside
    bays), the ceiling, stations short of room, corridor crossings, tunnel props per 100 studs, the longest stretch
    without one per side, and props in the running lane or its clearance. `NOTUNNEL=1` must give the IDENTITY hash from
    before any tunnel change. `IDENTITY no-tunnel tracks` and the per-track line show what a tunnel change moves
    elsewhere: the earth over a wider roof may re-snap a neighbour's props (Y only) beyond its corridor; corridor columns
    never change.
- **Lights**: one `SurfaceLight` or neon strip per `LightEvery` studs, `Shadows = false`. REDUCED EFFECTS halves the
  count (client hides every second light). Every `SIDE_LIGHTS`-th (3rd) strip also gets one strip over the middle of
  each side whose half-width is at least `SIDE_LIGHT_HALF` (40).
- **Zone lighting, underground (no sky)**: an Atmosphere profile flag `Underground = true`. The client then sets
  `CelestialBodiesShown = false`, `StarCount = 0`, a plain dark Skybox, `Brightness = 0`, `ClockTime = 0`, a low
  `OutdoorAmbient`, an interior `Ambient`, `EnvironmentDiffuseScale` and `EnvironmentSpecularScale` low, Clouds
  `Cover = 0`, so only the tunnel lights and `Ambient` light the scene. Weather events do not mix in while underground.
- **Terrain interaction** (owner 2026-10-31): the ridge stays solid ground and covers the tunnel. A corridor mode fills
  terrain above the ceiling, so neighbouring laps and the plaza see a hill, and the tunnel reads as dug into earth. The
  corridor rule "never above road Y - 1" holds only inside the tunnel box.
- **Camera** (owner 2026-10-31): the camera is clamped below the ceiling inside the tunnel; the track camera (design doc 4.12) must stay under the ceiling. `Height` must leave
  the camera room, or the ceiling must not block camera pop-in.
- **Dressing** (values placeholders): on a track whose biome has a `Tunnel`, `BiomeDressing` places every prop inside the
  tunnel, never at edge + `Offset` in the earth outside the walls.
  - A prop stands at road Y between the road edge + `CLEARANCE` and the wall at the adaptive width, or on a bay platform
    at road Y + `Tunnel.PLATFORM` (4) where a bay on its side covers its whole length. It keeps `TUNNEL_INSET` (1 stud)
    from the tunnel wall and the platform edge, plus 0.1 stud per stud of its half-length (`TUNNEL_TURN`: the walls are
    chords). Its box includes every fallback part's corners. Its top (plus half the road rise under it on a slope) stays
    under the ceiling. `Offset` is clamped into that room. A prop that does not fit, or lies outside the tunnel span, is
    not built. Ground patches are not built inside a tunnel.
  - Such props carry attribute `Tunnel = true`; `SnapToTerrain` leaves them where they are (no snap, no pad).
  - A layer with `Surface = true` (Rhythm, Landmarks, Horizon, Scatter or Blocks) opts out: its props are placed as on
    an open track and snap onto the earth over the tunnel. Use it only for sparse surface dressing such as vents and
    station entrances.
  - Side: side -1 is the tunnel's `Left` and 1 its `Right` (both from the same road tangent).
  - A pinned hero (`At`) stands at the waypoint nearest `At` (XZ length, like `Bays`), in the bay covering `At`, on
    that bay's side (`Both`: the side the hero rule gives). With no bay at `At` the server warns. A pinned hero that
    does not fit inside the tunnel is not built and the server warns (the 3.2 "never deleted" rule cannot put it in
    the earth; the builder moves or resizes it).
  - Gate: on a tunnel track without `Gate.Index` the gate moves back to the last waypoint whose posts end before the
    start portal (at least waypoint 2), so the gate never stands under the ceiling. A gate that would still stand
    inside the tunnel is not built and is reported. `Gate.Skip` still removes it.
  - Check: `TUNNEL=1 lune run tools/lune/biome_harness.luau` prints every tunnel prop inside / not inside, pinned
    heroes in their bay, and the gate distance before the portal. Non-tunnel biomes build identically (identity hash).
- **Ramps**: the tunnel hollow's floor sits under the lowest road within `FLOOR_REACH` (40) studs, lower by the steepest
  slope of the segments within that reach (crests and sags too) times `FLOOR_REACH`. The `Floor` slab covers it. Check:
  `lune run tools/lune/biome_harness.luau` prints `corridor_clear` per tunnel track (0 hits).
- **Tests**: test 1 (`terrain_built`) also checks no terrain voxel inside the tunnel box. `tunnel_clear`: every
  20 studs a ray up from the road hits the ceiling no lower than road Y + 12, and no wall is within `CLEARANCE` of a
  checkpoint, pad or WinPad. The track tests (0 flips, 0 off-track) still pass with the tunnel in place.

## 4. Meshes

### 4.1 Pipeline
1. **Make**, one lane per asset:
   - Lane A (preferred): Studio `generate_mesh`, `generate_procedural_model`, `generate_texture` or
     `generate_material`, in Edit mode on the Anime Run studio (never Racer RNG).
   - Lane B: a Blender MCP model (low-poly, flat or 512 px colour textures), published from Studio as the owner's
     asset.
   - Lane C (small props only, no upload): the existing `BiomeMeshCodec` / EditableMesh path near the camera.
   - Lane D (owner 2026-10-30): a free Roblox Creator Store asset (mesh, model, texture, decal, sound, particle
     texture). Free only; no paid or limited items. Inserted in Edit mode, every Script / LocalScript /
     ModuleScript removed, then the meshes are read for their ids. Its ledger row records `Creator` and the
     Creator Store asset id. It passes the same IP review (step 3); character-shaped or fan-made anime assets are
     rejected.
2. **Prompt rule**: a generation prompt never names a show, a character, a real place or a brand. It uses generic
   words ("wooden village gate, red tiled roof, low poly anime style"). The prompt is stored in the ledger, and the
   real-name check scans it.
3. **IP review** before an id is used:
   - Go through the section 5 list and the track's References IP flag: no logos, crests, village or school symbols,
     flags, skulls, show kanji, real names, iconic exact silhouettes (the H-shaped tower, the named ship, the real
     carved faces, the film castle or robot).
   - A second agent (the rules auditor) checks a screenshot of the asset and signs `Reviewed = "<date> <who>"`.
   - Failures are regenerated, never edited to hide a symbol.
4. **Upload** under the owner's account (owner 2026-10-30) and record the id in the biome module's `Assets` ledger.
5. **Remove** the scratch instances from the place after recording the id. Nothing in the place is a source of
   truth.

### 4.2 Registry (`BiomeAssets`, server)
- When the dressing reads a prop def with `Mesh = "<AssetKey>"`, the registry loads the asset with
  `AssetService:CreateMeshPartAsync(Content.fromUri(id))` (pcall) on first use. It caches one template per key and
  clones it per placement. It applies `TextureContent` from the ledger, sizes the clone to the def's `Size` and
  anchors it. `CanCollide`, `CanTouch` and `CanQuery` are off.
- A load failure, or an unreviewed asset, builds the def's `Parts` instead. Every mesh def keeps a parts fallback, so
  the world is complete with zero assets.
- `RenderFidelity = Automatic`, `CollisionFidelity = Box`. `CastShadow` follows section 8.

### 4.3 Landmarks, mid props, horizon
- **Hero landmarks**: 2-4 per biome (section 7).
  - Tall rather than wide, to fit the gap: footprint at most 60 x 60 studs, height 60-220 studs (placeholders).
  - One model each, at points along the track where the route faces them (corner exits and straights 200+ studs
    long).
  - Optional `At = 0.8` on a hero def (owner 2026-10-30) pins it to that fraction of the track length, snapped to the
    nearest corner exit or 200+ stud straight. Hero defs without `At` keep cycling by index.
  - `ModelStreamingMode = Atomic`, `LevelOfDetail = SLIM` (falls back to `StreamingMesh` if SLIM is not available),
    so they stay visible past the streaming radius. Set ahead of time (owner 2026-11-01): heroes clone
    `ReplicatedStorage.HeroLodTemplate`, a Model Rojo syncs with SLIM + Atomic, because runtime scripts cannot write
    LevelOfDetail.
  - Up to 20k triangles each (the per-mesh import cap; placeholder), one texture at 1024 px.
- **Mid props**: 4-8 unique per biome: one mesh instead of 3-12 parts for the most-repeated props (trees, houses,

## 5. Atmosphere (client only)

### 5.1 Zone detection
- `src/client/BiomeEnvironment.client.luau` reads the `BiomeZone_<n>` polylines once.
- Every 0.25 s (placeholder) it finds the nearest point to the camera focus (the runner while racing, the character
  otherwise). Zone 0 applies inside the plaza radius (attribute `PlazaRadius`, set by `TerrainShape`).
- A new zone must hold for 0.5 s (placeholder hysteresis) before the blend starts.
- High speed (owner 2026-11-01, design doc 4.18; placeholders): poll 0.1 s; hold `min(0.5, 40 / speed)`; blend
  `clamp(240 / speed, 0.4, 3)` s; the zone is looked up ahead along the focus velocity by `hold + poll + blend / 2`
  (only zone current + 1 accepted), so the midpoint sky swap lands near the boundary. Sky faces of the next zone are
  preloaded. At low speed the 0.5 s hold and 3 s blend are unchanged. Biome welcome popup (4.17) fires on blend start.
- The runner sits on its own track's centre line, so racing always gives the right zone.

### 5.2 Profile (data in the biome module; zone 0 = the plaza golden-hour profile, section 2.1)
```
Atmosphere = {
	Sky = { Skybox = "<AssetKey>"|nil, SunTextureId?, MoonTextureId?, SunAngularSize = 21, MoonAngularSize = 11,
	        StarCount = 3000, CelestialBodiesShown = true },
	Lighting = { ClockTime, Brightness, Ambient, OutdoorAmbient, ExposureCompensation, ColorShift_Top,
	             EnvironmentDiffuseScale, EnvironmentSpecularScale },
	Atmosphere = { Density, Offset, Color, Decay, Glare, Haze },
	Grade = { Brightness, Contrast, Saturation, TintColor },      -- Lighting.Grade (ColorCorrectionEffect)
	Bloom = { Intensity, Size, Threshold },                       -- Lighting.Bloom
	SunRays = { Intensity, Spread },
	Clouds = { Cover, Density, Color },                           -- Terrain.Clouds
	Water = { Color, Transparency, Reflectance, WaveSize, WaveSpeed }, -- Terrain.Water* (client-local)
	Particles = { { Texture, Color, Size, Rate, Lifetime, Speed, SpreadAngle, LightEmission, Area = Vector3 } },
}
```
- The client writes the existing replicated instances `Lighting.Grade`, `Bloom`, `SunRays`, `Atmosphere`, the `Sky`
  and `Terrain.Clouds` locally. It creates no second ColorCorrection.
- Local writes stay until the server writes the same property again. Only `WeatherService` does that. The client
  listens with `GetPropertyChangedSignal` on the properties weather writes and re-applies its blended value on the
  next frame.
- Weather on top of the biome: while `ReplicatedStorage.WeatherEvent` is an event with a `Sky` (`WeatherConfig`), the
  final value is `lerp(biome, weatherTarget, 0.5)` (placeholder) for `ClockTime`, `Brightness`, `OutdoorAmbient`,
  Atmosphere and Clouds. A Night Race event darkens a day biome, and a night biome stays night.
- Particles: one client-only `Part` (`CanCollide`, `CanQuery` and `CanTouch` off, `Transparency = 1`), size `Area`,
  kept 20 studs (placeholder) ahead of the camera. It holds at most 2 emitters per profile, with Rate at most 40 per
  emitter (placeholder). Textures are in-house, made with the roblox-vfx method (numpy-drawn, uploaded, listed in the
  ledger).

### 5.3 Blend
- Every numeric and colour property lerps from its current value to the target over `BLEND_SECONDS = 3`
  (placeholder), with a sine ease, per frame.
- A Sky texture cannot lerp. At the blend midpoint Atmosphere `Haze` peaks at +1.5 and `Density` at +0.15
  (placeholder), and the Skybox ids swap under the haze.
- Particles fade their Rate out over the first half and in over the second.
- A new zone during a blend starts from the current mid-blend values.

## 6. Audio (client only)
- Source: in-house loops and one-shots. Agents make them with python3 (numpy filtered noise, tones, layered and
  looped seamlessly, 30-60 s, OGG) in `assets/audio/<BiomeKey>/` and upload them under the owner's account (owner
  2026-10-30). Ids go in the ledger. Free Creator Store sounds (Lane D, owner 2026-10-30) are allowed too, with
  the same ledger row (id + creator) and no official show audio.
- Per biome: 1 loop and at most 2 one-shots. One-shots play at random every 10-25 s (placeholder), at random
  positions 30-80 studs from the camera, volume 0.15-0.25.
- Loop volume is 0.3 (placeholder, per-biome override 0.2-0.45). The sounds live in a client-made `SoundGroup`
  "Ambience" (Volume 1) under `SoundService`.
- Two Sound objects A/B crossfade over `BLEND_SECONDS`, in step with the atmosphere blend.
- `SoundService.AmbientReverb` is set per zone (client-local) at the blend midpoint.
- The existing Settings SOUND toggle already mutes every Sound in the game (`RacerUiController` `applySound` on
  `DescendantAdded`). No new mute is needed.
- A missing id gives silence for that layer, never an error.
