# DS series: accepted departures and open points (internal, never ships)

Shared status and look facts: `docs/CHARACTER_ART_REFERENCES.md`.

- `ds_hero`: source earrings are a hanafuda card with red sun and rays, and Rising Sun-style elements were retouched in China; the model uses the ledger mark. Forehead scar exists in the design; shape and side not described.
- `ds_sister`: younger sister of `ds_hero`, treated as a child: fully covered, high collar, kimono to the shins. Hair to the thighs (references say knees).
- `ds_thunder`: the source's yellow sword shows only as a yellow handle; the blade stays sheathed.
- `ds_boar`: bare torso kept from the references: a plain skin block with no anatomy drawn.
- `ds_flame`: plain round sword guard, not the flame shape (source shape not described).
