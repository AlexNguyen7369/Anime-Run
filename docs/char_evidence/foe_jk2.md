## Contradictions and choices
- Eyes: grey in the manga, heterochromia in the anime. Chose the anime pair, matching the version.
- Eye side: the old model had the seam through the blue eye. The anime frame shows the long seam through the grey eye, which sources put on his right. The seam now runs through the grey eye.
- Top colour: one text says black, a licensed figure listing says blue, the anime frame shows blue-violet, the figure photos near-black navy. Chose a dark navy, which sits between them and keeps the grid readable.
- Grid: the anime-style listing art draws pale lines, the figure sculpts dark raised ridges. Chose a painted raised line, muted pale, with a dark groove beside it. The look-alike art breaks its lines into dashes; these run unbroken.
- Neck: the old model had a deep V. Every image viewed shows a round neck. Round neck used.
- Shoes: one page says "a single white shoe". Every image viewed shows a pair. A pair used.
- Section placement: the figure swings its hanks over the shoulders in action poses and has an alternate back piece. Chose the listing art's rest pose with two sections on the chest, plus the sourced one at the back.

## Project rules that override the source
- Plain block hands with no fingers (design doc section 5). The source's hands are not attempted.
- The trousers are slim in the source. The blocky R15 leg is never slimmed, so they stay block width.
- The source's muscled forearm and thin build are not sculpted: chunky block arms, head about 1/3 of body height.
- The block figure has no neck, so the neck seam sits on the skin of the top of the chest, inside the round neck.
