## Contradictions and choices

- The brief's default says a ponytail. The wiki and the portrait show a shoulder-length bob with a side fringe. No source reached gives a ponytail. Chosen: bob. Owner may rule otherwise.
- Eyes: grey, blue or hazel across sources. Chosen: grey, supported by the portrait.
- Sword point-down and out rather than point-up: it keeps the portrait frame on her face, and the sword still reads from three of the four views. Its arm's bounding box grows.

## Project rules over source

- Chunky R15 block body with no slimming or taper (design doc section 5).
- Plain block hands, no fingers.
- The block body stays unslimmed; femininity comes from the bob, the face and lashes.
- The chest uses plain gold lines, no emblem.
