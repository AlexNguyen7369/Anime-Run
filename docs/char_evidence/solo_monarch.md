## Contradictions and choices

- Version change (owner 2026-11-05, "should look like his more casual self with a cardigan"): long coat, belt, boots and Shadow Monarch outfit removed. Hair and face kept unchanged, as instructed.
- No canon scene with a cardigan was reached. The search found no source for a specific casual outfit in the source work; the cardigan rests on the owner's instruction and one fan costume guide. Owner may have a specific scene in mind.
- Hood: the guide says hooded. Not modelled: the hair covers the neck and the owner asked for a cardigan only.
- Second revision (owner 2026-11-05, "should have black cardigan and carrying his twin blades daggers. redo hair for him as well"): cardigan black, daggers back, hair rebuilt. The first revision's charcoal and dagger removal are superseded.
- Colour: black cardigan as owner and guide say. Lighter grey rib bands, yoke seam and back folds, a white tee and indigo jeans keep it from merging into one dark mass.
- Hair: the first drafts' tall spikes replaced by tiers of wide locks, because the portrait shows a soft messy shape and the old one read like the roster's spiky-hair characters.
- Third revision (owner 2026-11-05, "do research on jinwoo, he should have his daggers from the anime. Also redo hair to be closer to ip"): generic curved daggers replaced, hair rebuilt against anime art. Earlier hair and dagger notes are superseded.
- Daggers chosen: Knight Killer in his right hand and Kasaka's Venom Fang in his left, the two he holds in the Episode 12 still and in the anime's official character art. Most recognisable because the anime's own design art shows them; the red toothed blade is the one on his design sheet.
- Runner-up: Demon King's Daggers, Episode 24. They are the show's true matched twins and the owner's word "twin" may mean these, but no anime image of them was reached. The owner should rule. The chosen two are a pair by use, not matched.
- Daggers with a cardigan: no source shows this combination. Owner instruction.
- Hair differences from the previous draft, each fixed: (1) a wide dome with side tufts, now a closer rounded mass; (2) a fan of even bangs from one root, now split locks with skin between them and one long lock between the eyes; (3) ears buried, now showing; (4) a flat back block, now shingled locks with alternating tone over a shell to a tapered nape; (5) blue-slate in the portrait, now neutral black through a slightly warm hair ramp, because the portrait grade adds blue to dark values.
- Eyes: text says grey, the first brief says glowing. Glow kept because the revision says to keep the face, but glowing eyes sit oddly with an everyday look. Owner may prefer plain grey.
- Glow is painted into the face; there is no particle effect in this draft.

## Project rules over source

- Chunky R15 block body with no slimming or taper (design doc section 5).
- Plain block hands, no fingers.
- Dark colours: the hair ramp's top stop is a cool grey so facets read on a grey background; hair stays black at distance.
- Daggers are chunky faceted flat layers modelled in-house, oversized from the source so they read at game camera, held point-up. Portrait shows only the upper blades; the cut under the chest removes the guards and grips.
- The cardigan's panels below the waist ride the legs and stay at z <= 2.
- Jeans are loose, not skinny: the block body and trouser stay unslimmed.
