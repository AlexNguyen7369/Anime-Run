## Marks

None. The version has no emblem, badge, hairpin or symbol tattoo in any source read, so nothing needed a shape change. Not listed in `docs/CHARACTER_ART_REFERENCES.md` for that reason.

## Contradictions and choices

- Starting notes said "white high-collared robe"; the source read says "coat". Same garment, modelled as a long coat.
- A search excerpt of the fan wiki describes the same hair change as a hand swept through the hair; no conflict in the result.
- The earlier captain era (glasses, soft hair, haori) was left out on purpose. Tags such as "neck-length hair" describe that era.
