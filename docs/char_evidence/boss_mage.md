## Contradictions and choices
- Golem: the exam's bottled escape golems (episode 23) are a different thing from the rubble golem of the fight (episode 26). Chosen: the fight's golem, with the look of the owner's reference 6.
- Barrage colour: the replica's blast is described as black in episode 26; her own is white. Chosen: white and blue (owner's request).
- Lightning's first use: one snippet says episode 21, the others 25-26. Not needed for the design.
