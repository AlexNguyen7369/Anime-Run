# boss_mage evidence

Id `boss_mage`, display name The Elf Mage (owner 2026-11-11). Based on: Frieren, Frieren: Beyond Journey's End (Sousou no Frieren). Version: season 1 travelling outfit, as in the First-Class Mage Exam arc (episodes 24-26, the fight with her replica in the Ruins of the King's Tomb). Companion id `boss_golem`: the stone golem of that fight.
Usage: public Roblox experience (boss event). Rig: bespoke named parts on a blocky R15 body, staff separate. Check date: 2026-11-11.

Licensing: no Roblox-supported license found (Roblox License Manager lists Netflix, SEGA, Lionsgate, Kodansha; no Shogakukan, Toho or Aniplex entry). Unlicensed reference. Roblox anime games have received DMCA takedowns. A tweaked name is not a license; the owner decides on upload (design doc section 5).

## Contradictions and choices
- Golem: the exam's bottled escape golems (episode 23) are a different thing from the rubble golem of the fight (episode 26). Chosen: the fight's golem, with the look of the owner's reference 6.
- Barrage colour: the replica's blast is described as black in episode 26; her own is white. Chosen: white and blue (owner's request).
- Lightning's first use: one snippet says episode 21, the others 25-26. Not needed for the design.

## Owner reference clips (6, 2026-11-12, look-only, never ship)
Screen recordings saved by the owner in `../AnimeRun-orch/art-backup/anime_refs/boss_mage/` (outside the repo). Read as contact sheets at 4 and 10 frames per second; times are from the clip start, accurate to one sheet frame. Clips 1, 2 and 4 show the apprentice mage casting the same spells; clip 5 is a different drawing style (source not identified). Staging and timing only: no frame, sound or drawing is copied.
- Clip 1 (9.2 s), single shot then many: the staff swings in a wide arc (0.25 to 1.0 s), body and skirt turning with it and the hair trailing one beat behind. A magic circle opens in front of the staff head at about 1.5 s and the beam leaves it within 0.25 s, with a short hold on the cast pose. From 3.25 s circles of different sizes pop in one after another: 1, 3, 6, then about 10 by 4.5 s. At 6.0 s all fire at once as straight white-blue beams; 6.5 s one white frame; 7.0 s on, flat white flower-shaped bursts on dark blue.
- Clip 2 (8.2 s), lightning: 0 to 0.25 s a bright point with a cross flare at the staff tip. From 0.5 s thick white-violet lightning fills the frame for about 2.5 s; the caster is a dark silhouette against it, braced, hair blown back, staff held out level.
- Clip 3 (24 s), golem and lightning: 0 to 1.5 s floor slabs crack and lift; 1.75 to 4 s blocks fly in and close round her into a body, head block last. 6 s a foot slam: a white dust cloud with flat shock rings and thrown chips, hanging for 2 s. 8.25 to 9.75 s a small crackle builds at the staff tip, then one thick bolt (10.5 s). 12 to 17 s branching bolts run from the caster to the target with purple smoke clouds lit from inside; 20 to 21.5 s a last burst, a bright frame, then grey dust with slow sparkles.
- Clip 4 (10 s), barrage: 3 large circles stand in front of the staff, tilted to face the shot; each fires a thick beam with a rounded head (1.25 to 2.75 s). Then 3 parallel beams held on one spot for about 2.5 s with a pale smoke cloud at the hit. Ends on the feet settling after the cast.
- Clip 5 (3.1 s): white orbs on curved trails; the caster braces the staff in both hands while spiky white bursts flash on and off about every 0.2 s (a strobe during the sustained cast).
- Clip 6 (2.1 s): circles pop in one at a time, 1 to 6 over about 0.8 s, then dozens fill the sky by 1.3 s; a dark beat, then a white burst.
- Still (1, `Screenshot 2026-11-12 at 1.46.03 AM.png`, the end of clip 6): the beam's hit on a standing figure. The body is eaten from the chest outward by a flat white-blue glow with a soft halo, the rest of the figure stays in dull grey-blue, and the background drops to a dark fog. The hit reads from the contrast (one bright shape on a dimmed room), not from many particles.
- The 10 numbered images `1.png` to `10.png` in the same folder are the 2026-11-11 look references listed above (model, room, golem, circle), not animation references.
- Taken for the build: a bright point at the staff tip before lightning; a dimmed room with one bright white shape at the moment a beam lands; her body as a dark, braced shape against the strikes during the 2 s window; circles opening one by one, small to large, in front of the staff and facing the shot; beams with a rounded head; a single white frame at the first strike; dust with shock rings for the golem's rise, slam and fall; hair and tails trailing one beat behind the staff.
- Not taken: the honeycomb barrier and the tentacles (not her moves), any drawn frame.
