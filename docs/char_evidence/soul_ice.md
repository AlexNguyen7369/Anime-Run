## Starting notes checked

- "Short black hair": holds as neck length. Fan sources also describe a later bob, which is a different era.
- "A strand between the eyes": holds. Sources disagree on the count: one strand (kakashi434 blog) against several strands (mywaifulist). The model uses one wedge, matching the images viewed.
- "Black shihakusho robes", "white tabi": hold.
- "White sword": holds for the released form (Wikipedia, kakashi434 blog). A search summary with no named source gives the sealed form a reddish-brown hilt and a rectangular guard, so white is taken to mean shikai.

## Contradictions and mixed form

- The sheath at the hip is left out, because its look is not verified. The model shows only the released sword.
- The waist tie is called a cord, a belt and an obi across sources. All agree on white; the model uses a flat band.
- Mixed form: none intended. The released sword belongs to the same pre-skip era as the uniform, but the claim that it appears pre-skip rests on memory of the series, not on a source opened here.

## Source limits

- bleach.fandom.com answered 402 and wikiwand.com and giantbomb.com answered 403. They were not retried with a disguised client. No attribute above is credited to those sites. The search tool only returned unattributed summaries over mixed result lists, so those count as agreement, not as sources.
- No official publisher or licensed guide was reached. Supported rows rest on Wikipedia, fan wikis, a fan blog, a forum and a fan database, so they are weak.
- No full-body image of the uniform was viewed. Lower-body details rest on text alone.
- That image 516626 shows the pre-skip look is my reading of it; the page does not label its era.

## Marks

None. The pre-skip standard uniform carries no emblem, badge or symbol hairpin visible on the outside. A lieutenant badge is reported only for the later era, by a search summary with no named source. The division emblem sits inside the kosode (https://daddyjim.com/bleach/item/Shihakusho). The sword guard is treated as a part of the sword, not a logo.
