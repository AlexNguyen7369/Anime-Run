## Contradictions and choices

- Eye colour: yellow in the manga, red in the anime. Brief says yellow-red. Chosen: yellow ball, red ring, black dot, so both show.
- Hair and wings: the page says black hair and wings belonged to a discarded first concept, and it does not describe wings on the final design. The owner's brief asks for both and the illustration shows the hair, so the brief is followed.
- Skin: brief asks for pale blue-white; the page says bluish grey. Chosen: a pale blue ramp, between the two.
- Earrings on both sides for symmetry. Source shows one. Owner may prefer one.

## Project rules over source

- Chunky block body and short limbs in place of the source's very long, gaunt limbs (design doc section 5). This is the largest departure.
- Plain block hands, no fingers or claws.
- No notebook, apple or series symbol modelled. No marks entered in the ledger.
