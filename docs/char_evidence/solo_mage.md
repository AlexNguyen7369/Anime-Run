## Contradictions and choices

- The brief's default says grey hair. Every source reached says red, and the portrait shows dark red. Chosen: dark red. Owner may rule otherwise.
- Red cape or long coat: the brief says coat, the sources say cape. Chosen: a long crimson coat worn open with sleeves, because a block arm reads better in a sleeve. Owner may prefer a sleeveless cape.
- Eight rings reduced to one gold band on each plain block hand, since there are no fingers.

## Project rules over source

- Chunky R15 block body with no slimming or taper (design doc section 5).
- Plain block hands, no fingers.
- Glasses are painted on the face block with temple arms on the head sides, so they stay crisp at thumbnail size.
