## Marks

Same place, colours and about the same size, a clearly different shape.
- Chest sun: red straight-spiked star, 8 long and 8 short points, hollow ring and crescent centre, instead of flame rays round a disc. Matches the existing line in `docs/CHARACTER_ART_REFERENCES.md`.
- Forearm crew tattoo: tall red lozenge with a dark core and two pairs of straight chevrons, instead of curved hooks and flames. In the ledger in `docs/CHARACTER_ART_REFERENCES.md`; this task could not edit that file.

## Contradictions and choices

- The earlier quick model had a straw hat, a flower shirt, an orange sash, brown shorts and chest gills. No source read supports any of them; all are gone.
- Gills: no image viewed shows them and no text read mentions them, so none are modelled. They may exist in pages that refused reading.
- Skin: anime images are pale lavender-blue, the figure is saturated blue. A periwinkle between them.
- Sash: purple in the anime art and the Funko text, blue on the figure. Purple.
- Shorts: olive in the anime art, near-black green on the figure. Olive.
- Sandals: brown in a game render, grey on the figure, cropped in the anime art. Brown, low confidence.
- Nose teeth: underneath in IMG-A, on the upper edge in IMG-D. Teeth on both side edges and underneath, so it reads as a saw from every view. The blade rises more than IMG-A's almost level nose, so it stays readable from the front.
- Hair: one figure database field says blue hair; text and every image say dark or black. Black.
- Grin: IMG-C's closed interlocking teeth, not the open roar of IMG-A and IMG-D, which shows blocky upper teeth and a pink tongue.

## Departures for project rules

- Finger rings not modelled: hands are plain blocks with no fingers (design doc 5, Target look).
- Figure stays on the standard R15 block body, so 263 cm and the long limbs are not modelled (never slim or taper; same proportions as the cast).
- Weapon not modelled: this task did not ask for it and the anime art viewed shows him without it. The owner may want it.
