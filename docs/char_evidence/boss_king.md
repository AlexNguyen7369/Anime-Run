# boss_king evidence

Id `boss_king`, display name The Golden King (owner 2026-11-11). Based on: Gilgamesh, Fate/stay night (Archer form, the Unlimited Blade Works route and film). Sidekick id `side_king`: the same design downsized (`side_king.md`). The real name appears only in `docs/`, never in ids, titles, asset names or product names (design doc section 5).
Usage: public Roblox experience (boss event, design doc 4.37 "Third boss", `docs/BOSS_EVENT_SPEC.md` "The Golden King"). Rig: bespoke named parts on a blocky R15 body (never slimmed; hair, face and paint carry the likeness), Ea and the key as separate parts. Check date: 2026-11-11.

Licensing: Roblox License Manager names no Fate / Aniplex / Type-Moon licence (checked 2026-11-11). Unlicensed reference. Roblox anime games have received DMCA takedowns. A tweaked title is not a license; upload needs the owner's word, as for Saitamo and The Elf Mage (design doc 4.35, 4.37).

## Contradictions and choices
- Armor against the shirtless Ea pose: the sources show both. Chosen: full golden armor for the model; the red markings are not modelled (the armor covers them) unless the owner asks for the shirtless Enuma Elish pose.
- Gate projectile motion: the series fires straight lines, the UBW film adds curved streams. Chosen: straight lines across the arena (owner 2026-11-11, "not rain"), with trails.
- Shields: no canonical set exists. Chosen: assorted vault shields, 5-7 (**proposed**), each a different look.
- Hair variants: some art shows the hair down (casual form). Chosen: spiky upswept (Archer form).

## Owner reference clips (3, 2026-11-12, look-only, never ship)
Screen recordings saved by the owner in `../AnimeRun-orch/art-backup/anime_refs/boss_king/` (outside the repo). Read as contact sheets at 4 and 10 frames per second; times are from the clip start and are accurate to one sheet frame (0.1 s or 0.25 s). Staging and timing only: no frame, sound or drawing is copied.
- Clip 1 (4.2 s), Gates opening: face close-up 0 to 0.6 s, camera pulls back to a full figure by 1.2 s, he stands still. First portal at about 1.6 s; 3 portals by 1.8 s, about 10 by 2.3 s, 25 or more by 2.7 s (a cascade that doubles roughly every 0.25 s). Portals start as a small bright dot, grow into a ring with a hot core, small ones first. At 2.8 s a wide shot shows about 50 portals with star glints; the room goes dark and the portals light it. His body does not move: the stillness is the pose.
- Clip 2 (25.7 s), Enuma Elish: 0 to 5.5 s Ea in close-up, drums turning, grooves glowing red, red wind streaks across the frame. 5.75 to 10 s white-pink lightning crawls over the blade and the frame. About 10.25 s he raises Ea overhead on one straight arm. 12 to 15 s a white ball builds at the tip with red rings swirling round it and lightning leaving it. 15.0 s one full white frame, then a horizontal flare line until 16.5 s. 16.75 to 19.5 s face close-up (the call). 19.5 to 19.75 s the downward swing as a smear frame. 20.0 to 20.5 s flat red and yellow frames with him as a small silhouette (impact frames, two colours only). 20.75 to 23 s the wall of red and yellow fire fills the frame; 23.5 s on, the beam tears across the ground with a dark red edge.
- Clip 3 (18 s), mass volley: portals are concentric ring spirals; they open behind and above the target in a dome of 50 or more. Weapons come out tip first, then fly as straight gold streaks with sparkle dust. The target raises a dome of small gold sparks; hits are a white cross flare, then an orange explosion with a dark outline. Ends on an eye close-up.
- Taken for the build: the growing portal cascade and the room dimming under the portals' light; drums and groove glow during the charge; a white ball and rings at Ea's tip; one white flash frame before the beam; a two-colour impact frame on the swing; streaks with sparkle dust.
- Not taken: the shirtless pose and red markings, the jacket (the model keeps its armor), any drawn frame.
