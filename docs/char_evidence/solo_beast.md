## Contradictions and choices

- The brief's default says brown hair. Sources say orange; the portrait shows a dark rust red-orange. Chosen: rust, keeping him clear of the orange-haired `soul_substitute`.
- Suit or raid gear: chosen suit with gold claws, the brief's claw hint. White hair and fur of his beast form not used. Owner may prefer the armour.

## Project rules over source

- Chunky R15 block body with no slimming or taper (design doc section 5).
- Plain block hands, no fingers.
- Claws are gold blades on a band round the fist: the hand itself stays a plain smooth block, no fingers.
