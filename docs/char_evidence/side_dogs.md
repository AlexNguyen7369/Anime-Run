## Contradictions and choices

- White or black: the owner ruled both, in one Sidekick slot (2026-11-05). White stands on the viewer's right in the front view, black on the left. The black dog shares the white dog's build.
- Black fur: lifted from pure black to slate planes, so it stays visible on dark backgrounds. It reads a little lighter than the source's dark fur.
- Black dog's markings: pale ash blaze on the chest, ash stripe along each flank, pink-grey inner ears. These are our choice for telling the dogs apart. No source names them.
- Forehead patterns: a single fan page; the stills reached do not show them clearly. Chosen: left out, with a plain pale shade point on each brow. Owner may rule otherwise.
- Pale markings (brief): lilac-grey shade on fur points, streaks on the muzzle and a band over each eye, read from the stills' shading. No source names them as markings.
- Name: sources give several names for the pair. Only the display name above is used.

## Project rules over source

- Hard facets and painted shading replace the source's inked fur (design doc section 5).
- Paws are plain blocks. Small dark claws kept: they are not fingers, and they read as a wolf.
- Marks: none.
