## Contradictions and choices
- Top: one search result summary calls it a pocketed vest. The figure photos and the figure article show a long-sleeved shirt. Chose the shirt; the vest source was not read directly.
- Hair: "grayish-blue" in text, "white; light blue (former)" in the same page's infobox, saturated blue on one figure, near white on another. White is a later era, so it is out. Chose a pale sky blue between the two figures.
- Trouser colour: black on one figure, dark navy on the other. Chose black, as the article states.
- Source strength: the scars, wrinkles and eye colour rest on one aggregator page that cites no primary source. Not confirmed against the manga or the anime.

## Project rules applied over the source
- Slim source body becomes the chunky R15 block body (design doc section 5); nothing tapered.
- His own hands are plain blocks with no fingers. The worn hands keep fingers: they are the costume, and without fingers they do not read as hands. The block-hand rule's reach over worn hands is for the owner to rule on.
- Marks: none. He has no logo, emblem or symbol tattoo. Hands, blocks and cables are costume; scars and wrinkles are not marks.
