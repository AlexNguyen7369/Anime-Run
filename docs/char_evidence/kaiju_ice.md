## Contradictions and choices

- Gas mask in the AniList portrait, none on the figure. Chosen: no mask, it hides the face.
- Ice is kept to the rifle so the standard suit and the later suit are not mixed.

## Project rules over source

- Chunky R15 block body with no slimming or taper (design doc section 5).
- Plain block hands, no fingers.
- Hand-built in code; no ripped or downloaded asset, no text, no logo.
