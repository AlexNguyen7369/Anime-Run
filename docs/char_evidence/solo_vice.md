## Contradictions and choices

- Hair colour: light brown or cream. The AniList portrait shows mid brown. Chosen: light brown, as the brief also says.
- Armour or his plain green hunter gear (AniList portrait). Chosen: armour, the character's running joke and his most recognisable look.
- His later leaner look with facial hair is not used.

## Project rules over source

- Chunky R15 block body with no slimming or taper (design doc section 5).
- Plain block hands, no fingers.
- Pauldrons kept low so they do not cover the chin in the portrait; the source's bulk comes from garment width only.
