## Contradictions and choices

- Staff: P has three prongs and a ring, F a plain rod with round knobs. Chose P: its silhouette is more distinctive.
- Forearm rings: P shows gold and dark bronze, F mostly gold. Chose P.
- Expression: D says smirk, P and F show an open grin. Chose the grin: both figures agree and it reads at distance.
- Earlobe length: W and D say chest, P shows belly. Followed P.
- Wrap colour: the earlier model used a white wrap with no source found. Replaced by the figures' blue.
- Ring: P's large gold flowered ring is part of its throne display and is left out. The thin grey ring behind the drums is the one modelled.
- Rings on the fingers (P article) are left out: the project's plain block hands have no fingers.

## Marks (design doc section 5, listed in docs/CHARACTER_ART_REFERENCES.md)

- Drum heads: same place, size and black on pale; shape changed from a three-comma swirl to four straight-edged swept blades round a hub.
