## Contradictions and choices
- Headgear: the 2007 Wikipedia copy says "expensive-looking glasses", Baidu says goggles. Chose goggles, as the brief and the arc's look do.
- Tattoo colour: Baidu says indigo, memory says black. Chose near black with a little indigo.
- Allowed departure from the design: the block figure has no neck, so the neck marks sit at the top of the chest opening.
- Tattoos on the back, stomach and thighs are under the robe and are not painted.

## Marks (same place, size and colours; clearly different shape)
- Forehead tattoo: two stepped bars with a square notch and a chevron end, in place of the source's lines.
- Tattooed brow: a stepped wedge with a barb at the outer end, in place of the source's forked line.
- Chest and neck tattoo: three pairs of the same stepped bar, in place of the source's bands.
