## Contradictions and choices
- Headgear: the 2007 Wikipedia copy says "expensive-looking glasses", Baidu says goggles. Chose goggles, as the brief and the arc's look do.
- Tattoo colour: Baidu says indigo, memory says black. Chose near black with a little indigo.
- Goggle look: the earlier two square lenses contradicted the images, so one visor replaced them (design doc section 5 Likeness). Same place on the forehead.
- Chest blocks: seen only in later-era images, so they are not painted. Adding them to this Soul Society version would be a mixed form.
- Tattoos on the back, stomach and thighs are under the robe and are not painted.

## Owner rulings
- 2026-11-04: the tattoos are a body pattern, not symbol marks, like `jk_king` face lines and `foe_ds` body lines. They follow the source layout and shape, so the Marks rule (design doc section 5) does not change their shape. This replaces the earlier stepped bars and chevrons.
- 2026-11-04: owner-accepted departure: the source's neck tattoos stay at the top of the chest, because the block figure has no neck.

## Body pattern as painted
- Brow and forehead: drawn in-house, following the layout under "Brow and forehead tattoo shape" above.
- Top of the chest: three stacked wedges a side, drawn in-house, in the place given by the second ruling.
- Reference images were viewed only, from outside this repo. None is saved here and nothing was traced or copied into the atlas.
