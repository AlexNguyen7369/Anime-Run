## Starting notes checked
- Spiky orange hair: confirmed. Black robes: confirmed. Cleaver on the back: confirmed. White wrap: a cloth wrap is confirmed, its white colour is inferred.
- Badge is a mark: treated as one under design doc section 5. Its source look could not be confirmed from text.

## Contradictions and gaps
- The first search summary warned that sources disagree on the Shikai's colour in the anime. Black body with a steel edge is the agent's recollection and is unconfirmed.
- A comicbook.com list describes a large white sword: that is an earlier sealed form, not the Shikai, so it was not used. https://comicbook.com/anime/list/bleach-every-ichigo-form-explained-list
- The Target listing is a costume for his soul reaper outfit, so "white trim" and "white belt" describe that product, not an official model sheet.
