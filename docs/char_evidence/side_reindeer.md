## Contradictions and choices

- Hat: the later blue hat with pink disc also exists. Chosen: the classic pink hat, as the brief says.
- Shorts: the brief's red kept. Owner may confirm; a source portrait shows blue with a black top.
- Fur shade: source text and stills disagree across eras. Chosen: tan face and mid-brown body.
- Bare fur top: chosen in place of any era's shirt, so the chest fur shows.

## Project rules over source

- Head and hat are about 60% of the height, against the proposed 1/3 for characters. The source's big head is its identity, and the 1/3 is proposed and written for R15 figures.
- Blocky torso, arms and legs; hooves are plain blocks, no fingers.
- Marks: the X is modelled in-house as two crossing bars. It is not yet listed in `docs/CHARACTER_ART_REFERENCES.md`: outside this draft's files.
