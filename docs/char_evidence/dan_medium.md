## Contradictions and choices

- Bat: metal in one figure, wood in the other. Chosen: metal.
- The source's neckline is open. Chosen: a closed dark high top under the straps and vest; longer shorts. Nothing sexualised.
- The stick in her mouth in the AniList art is left out (Roblox maturity: smoking imagery).

## Project rules over source

- Chunky block body with no slimming or taper (design doc section 5).

- No text or logos.
- Modelled in code by looking at references; nothing traced, ripped or added to the repo.
