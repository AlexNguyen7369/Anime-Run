## Contradictions and choices

- Sources differ on form: a giant monstrous ant, or a humanoid armoured figure. Chosen: humanoid, as the brief and the shadow visual show.
- Glow colour: the eyes are purple in text, while the visual's lines are pale blue. Chosen: purple eyes, blue-violet lines.
- The visual shows a slim waist. Not followed (see below).
- The gaster may be an invention: it helps the ant read from the side and three-quarter views. Owner may prefer it removed.
- Wings are folded on the back as plates, because this Sidekick walks. They are part of Torso and do not flap.

## Project rules over source

- Chunky block body in place of the source's tall, lean figure (design doc section 5). This is the largest departure.
- Plain block hands, no fingers. Three claw blades grow from each hand to keep the sharp-claw identity. Owner may rule on this.
- No marks or emblems modelled; none entered in the marks ledger.
