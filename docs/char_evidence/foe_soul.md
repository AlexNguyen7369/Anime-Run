## Marks

- Number tattoo: left out of the visible model. The source's closed jacket covers that part of the chest; the images that show it have the jacket torn, which is a different state. Adding it would mix states. No shape was made, because nothing shows. If the owner wants it shown, it needs a changed glyph and a torn or open chest.
- No other logo, emblem or symbol in this version. Not marks under design doc 5, so they follow the source: helm, hole, teal lines, hair, clothes.

## Contradictions and choices

- Hole: one source gives sternum with an earlier throat position; COS shows the throat. Modelled at the top of the sternum, inside the small V.
- Tattoo: a cosplay shop excerpt says left hip; two read sources say left chest. Chest stands, and it is hidden as above.
- Jacket back: Baidu says "white on the front and black on the back". No back-view image was found. Read as a white outside and black inside, which agrees with the collar in the images. The back is modelled white, so this is unverified.
- Hair length: text says short, images show locks to the jaw and below. Images win on length.
- Helm size: images differ by artist; anime art shows a simpler dome. Modelled dome, rim, one horn and lobes; a second upright peak seen in MAL-7/529323 was dropped, since it read as a cat ear on the block head.
- Source is not slim here: its "slimmest build" is overridden by the project rule, never slim or taper the R15 body.
