## Contradictions and choices

- Hair: dark brown in the anime figures, magenta in the manga cover art. Chosen: dark chestnut, the anime read.
- Earrings: the manga art has printed triangles with words. Chosen: plain teal drops, no text.
- Psychic glow hands left out: powers are not part of a static draft.
