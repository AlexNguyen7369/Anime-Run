Id `side_curse`, proposed display name Rikka. Based on: Rika, Jujutsu Kaisen. Version: the Queen of Curses monster from the film Jujutsu Kaisen 0, not the human girl and not the later full-manifestation form.

Licensing: no Roblox-supported license found; unlicensed reference. The owner accepts the takedown risk (design doc section 5). A tweaked name is not a license.

## Defining features

From six photos of one licensed figure of the film's curse, with listing text. Looked at only; none are in the repo.

1. Lunging pose, head thrust forward from a deep chest, no neck.
2. The head is mostly an open mouth, wide at the top and narrowing to a V at the chin.
3. Thick finger-like lobes form a shell over the top of the mouth and curl onto the upper lip.
4. Long, thin, needle teeth, the longest at the top middle, shorter up the jaw.
5. Dark maroon gums, near-black hollow, muted purple tongue with a groove.
6. Long pale tendrils from the back of the head, with irregular black patches, running black at the rear roots.
7. Pale, cool blue-grey skin with grey hatching.
8. Huge shoulders and a ladder of heavy ribs over a shadowed belly.
9. Long thick arms, huge hands, long fingers, dark pointed nails; one arm high, one low and forward.
10. No legs: a black trailing tail, with black patches where the pale skin ends.

## Contradictions and choices

- Two monster forms exist: the film's mouth-headed curse, and a later form with a white hair-like spray and a closed oval head. The brief's huge mouth and many teeth fit the first. Chosen: the first.
- Some pages describe wings, a halo and long hair. These come from unverified fan pages. Not used.
- Removed as unreferenced (owner revision 2026-11-05): the gold rings, stitched seams and dull patch, gem-shaped tongue, stud-like spots, and the darkened limbs. The first brief asked for rings and patchwork; no reference reached shows them on the curse. Owner may ask for them back.
- Contrast now comes from the source's own colours: pale hide against the black tail, black patches, near-black mouth and shadowed belly.
- The small lower-body swirl at the figure's base is treated as part of its stand. The tail ends in a point and two wisps.

## Project rules over source

- A monster, so no R15 body. Facets and thick limbs follow the target look; the waist narrows, as in the source.
- Hands have long fingers and nails. They go against the plain no-finger hands of the target look, because they are a defining feature. Owner may rule on this.
- The shell and mouth are larger, and the tendrils and fingers shorter and thicker than the source, to read at 2.3 studs.
- No marks or emblems. None entered in the ledger.
