## Contradictions and choices

- Sources give red-orange, black or gold fur. Gold is the chakra mode; black came from an unreliable page. Chosen: orange, as the brief says.
- Source's forelimbs are arm-like, with long digits. Chosen: four chunky legs with block paws, as this is a walking Sidekick and must stay chunky.
- Chest ruff is paler than the fur, so the head separates from the body. The source's chest is near the body colour. Owner may prefer one colour.
- No seal, spiral or series mark modelled. One reference photo shows a seal display stand: it was ignored.

## Project rules over source

- Chunky, faceted forms in place of the source's fine fur and lean limbs.
- Block paws with spike claws, not digits. Owner may rule on this.
- No marks entered in the ledger.
