## Contradictions and choices

- Cape colour: the anime portrait is yellow-green, the full-body art and costume are a stronger green, text says "green". Chosen: a yellow-green between them, `#BCCB58`, leaning to the portrait.
- Lower legs: the costume has white boot covers, the full-body art has brown boots. Chosen: the art.
- Rim shape: the mug has a wide rim, the portrait a narrower flared one. Chosen: the portrait.
- Old model's black stitched band across the eye: no source supports it. Removed.
- Fan text "suit": the art reads as a tunic and baggy trousers. Chosen: the art.

## Project rules over source

- Standard block body, no stoop, no short squat proportions, no bowed legs (design doc section 5; R15 body never slimmed).
- Plain block hands, no fingers. No cane: not in the runner's kit. Flames left out: rage state only.
- No marks: blotches, drips and fur lines are cloth and skin, so no shape change applies.
