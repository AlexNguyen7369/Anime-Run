## Contradictions and choices

- Scarf colour: red in some text, white in the anime per the wiki, orange-red in the infobox art. Chosen: orange-red, the most recognisable.
- Hair: one search summary placed the long hair only on a later form; the wiki and the infobox art show it here too. Chosen: long.
- Bracers: the brief says gold bracers, the source has dark gloves with gold bands. Chosen: dark gloves with gold bands and studs.
- Lower body: source has full legs. The brief asks for hover with floating lower body parts. Chosen: separate hips, thighs and shins trailing behind, with a small gap at each knee. Owner may rule on this reading.
- Hair is modelled thicker and shorter than the source, so it reads as a mass at 2 studs.

## Project rules over source

- Chunky block body in place of the source's tall, sculpted figure (design doc section 5). Muscle is block plates on the full-width torso.
- Plain block hands, no fingers.
- Modelled from text and by looking at references. The spirals are plain rings, not traced. Owner may want them in the marks ledger; not entered.
