## Marks

- Iris writing: same place and colour, dark on gold. Shape changed to invented glyphs: a kite with a tail, and two stacked hooks. Not real characters.
- Coat back pattern: same place and colours, dark plum on plum. Shape changed from squares to rows of lozenges.

The body and face lines are a line-pattern tattoo, not a mark, so they follow the source layout (project rule). Both marks match `docs/CHARACTER_ART_REFERENCES.md`.

## Contradictions and choices

- Tie colour: OTA says blue, DJ says turquoise. FZ, GEM and DJI show teal, so teal.
- Skin: AUR says "bright green", OTA "very pale", DJ "pale green-white". FZ shows pale mint and GEM a warmer pale, so pale green-white. AUR's wording is a poor rewrite and reads as a slip.
- Nails: OTA and AUR say orange, TOY says blood-red. FZ and GEM show orange-red, so orange.
- Hair: AUR says swept back, others say puffed. Figures show a short tousled puff, so puffed.
- QTA lists eyes as pink and yellow; OTA and DJ give yellow eyes with pink lashes elsewhere. Yellow iris, pink lashes.
- Face line path: OTA and AUR differ in wording (AUR has one line under the eyes). OTA's three curved lines each side match the figures, so OTA.
