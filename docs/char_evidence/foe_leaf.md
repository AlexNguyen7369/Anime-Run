## Contradictions and choices

- Forehead: the earlier quick model left it bare. Images show the band and plate, so the band and plate are modelled.
- Hair colour: Wikipedia's caption says "red-haired" for the group of six; the wiki summary and images show orange on this body. Orange chosen.
- Nose hardware: the portrait 73473 and the frame 413035 show six separate studs; the shirtless image 101035 looks like joined bars. Separate studs chosen, matching the "three studs" wording.
- Studs on the cheeks in image 597279 belong to a different Path; left out as a mixed form.
- Height and face length: the source is slim and long-faced. Project rules beat this: the blocky R15 body and big head stay.

## Marks (design doc section 5)

- Forehead plate symbol: same place, size and dark colour; four vertical lines become three leaning teardrops. The scratch stays, as on `leaf_crow`.
- Cloak clouds: same red and white, same kind of placement; the shared in-house shape, three lobes on a flat base, no inner line.
- Ear hardware (treated as earrings): same place, size and dark colour; round studs become square, the straight bar becomes a hooked bar.
- Not marks, so they follow the source: nose studs, lip spikes, eyes, hair, clothes.
