## Contradictions and choices
- Hairpin colour: listings disagree (blue; "silver" in one cosplay listing; "golden" on one The Japan Box page). The owner's starting note says blue; kept blue. The one drawn image I could open shows them small and pale, without a clear hue.
- Vest: the class art shows a beige sweater vest with a crest over the shirt, and a cosplay listing includes a vest. Left out: the owner's note asks for a pale uniform and the shirt-and-bow look is the one the listings agree on. No crest is modelled.
- Jacket: a grey jacket also exists in the listings. Not used, for the same reason.
- Hairpin place: a later period moves them to her collar (after Aizen's downfall, https://daddyjim.com/bleach/character/Orihime-Inoue). This version predates that, so they stay in the bangs.
- Hueco Mundo: she is known to wear another outfit there (cosplay listings at https://www.ebay.de/itm/405687007669, title only). The uniform here is the one from before that.
- Bleach Wiki (bleach.fandom.com) and a second fan wiki answered 402, eBay 403, and neither was retried or worked around. No official databook or publisher page was opened, so no attribute above has a primary source.
