# World 2 Track 3 `RuinedCityZ`: source references

Source: One Punch Man (anime season 1, manga). Look-only references for the Blender draft
`tools/blender/world2/biomes/RuinedCityZ.py`. The images sit in `build/world2/refs/RuinedCityZ/` (gitignored). They
never ship and nothing is traced or copied from them. Every prop is built in-house from primitives. No text and no
official logos or symbols (design doc 5).

Not matched 1-1: the hero's real apartment has more balcony units per floor and a separate outside stair. The draft
keeps 3 units per floor and leaves out the stair, so the block stays inside the tri budget. Fubuki-arc details (burn
marks) are left out.

Round 2 (2026-11-23): the hero block is now one unique instance. Buildings are warm grey with streaks, smashed panes and boarded shutters. The cordon board is tall and white with a red header. The overpass has a girdered underside and a fence. The crater has a raised berm rim. The HQ sits where the 33% and 62% views meet. Street-grid tiles were added.

Round 4 (2026-11-23): the HQ was rebuilt against both refs: a broad round base with a sheer slotted rim wall and a pale deck dotted with low blocks, a ribbed truncated octagonal hull, and a dense stepped block cluster on top, tallest in the middle. Twelve wide highways on piers radiate out. It is placed by a search, centred in the 33% view and inside the 62% view. The crater is now a tall ragged berm with asphalt slabs thrust up on the crest and toppled building stumps in a black bowl. A second crater sits beside the road in the 62% view. Half-collapsed blocks now show a sheared-off half with floor slabs hanging off the edge, plus a bare two-storey frame with rebar. The apartment stands farther from the start camera, so the whole end wall with its roof railing and the balcony stack are both in frame. The expressway crossing moved from 63% to 72% so it does not hide the HQ. Every prop is kept 24+ studs from the centre line by a measured post-pass.

Round 5 (2026-11-23): two crater refs were added (`Z-City_Street_Crater.ref.jpg` from the fandom file `Crater.png`, and `Meteor_fragment_aftermath.ref.jpg`). The ground cannot be cut, so the crater is rebuilt as a street heaved up around a pit. A grey asphalt apron with lane dashes rises to a lip of flat, overhanging broken asphalt plates. Inside, dark earth walls drop to a black floor, with a broken sewer pipe and a leaning wrecked block on the rim. The apartment's balconies now stand 9 out from the wall, each with an AC unit showing above its brick parapet. Railed corner balconies were added at the far end of both end walls. The block is turned 45 degrees to the start camera. The HQ highways are wide and pale, with lane dashes and curving branches, and the HQ stands on a brown cratered plain. The expressway fence is now a chain-link mesh with barbed coils.

Round 6 (2026-11-23): the crater matches `Z-City_Street_Crater.ref.jpg` more closely. The rim now stands 38 high. The pit walls are near-vertical stacks of dark earth strata, and thick asphalt plates overhang the hole. A sewer pipe about a quarter of the pit's width is set into the back wall. A low notch in the rim faces the 62% camera, so the pit shows from the road. The expressway now stands on the ground, and its chain-link fence has posts in footings, rails and barbed arms. The HQ stands dead ahead of the 33% view and fills the end of the street. The run views at 6% and 90% gained a dense street kit: utility poles with wires, street trees, vending machines, and low railing walls with shrubs.

Round 7 (2026-11-23): the crater was reshaped live in Blender against `Z-City_Street_Crater.ref.jpg`. The conical berm is gone. The pit now sits in a flat-topped asphalt plateau that tilts slightly toward the road camera. A lane dash and two kerbs with a reddish sidewalk run up to the lip. Nine large, irregular asphalt plates overhang the hole, tilted down into it. The pit walls are jagged wedge stacks in grey-brown, umber and near-black, with no grid. A light-grey sewer pipe juts from the right wall, turns down at an elbow and ends in a wide bell collar. The apartment's end wall is now cool mid-grey with darker grey patches and vertical stain streaks. Its balconies gained dark soffits and black recesses. The AC units sit on the parapets, and three grey rolled shutters run along the street front. The HQ hull now spans 75% of the deck radius, the rim wall is twice as tall, and each highway enters the rim through a dark slot. The mid-rises are now cool white and the high-rises light grey; the half-collapsed blocks are brick. About 60% of the facades are white or grey. An intact mid-rise leans 20 degrees along the street onto a high-rise neighbour in the 90% view, with rubble at its base (`Meteor_fragment_aftermath.ref.jpg`).

Round 8 (2026-11-23): the crater was rebuilt live in Blender as a deep, narrow pit. Its walls stand 46 high around a pit 80 wide, so they are more than half its width. The street is a raised asphalt plateau with a dark earth body. Its front is broken down to a low street lip, with a lane dash and kerbs running to the hole. That notch faces the road camera, so the dark walls and the pipe show from the road. The second crater stands closer to the 62% view, at 1.25 scale. Seven large grey asphalt plates overhang the hole. The walls are mottled stacks of dark grey-brown, umber and near-black earth. The sewer pipe is now about a quarter of the pit's width, light grey, with an elbow and a wide bell collar on the right wall. The HQ hull is now a truncated hull with a wide flat top. A broad, dense block cluster covers about 60% of that top and steps up to a block in the middle; the thin spire is gone. The apartment's end walls carry 4 large darker rectangular patches in place of the vertical streaks. The balconies stand 11 out from the wall, with deep near-black soffits. The mid-rise outside stair core is now a plain core with slit windows, so the zigzag flights are gone. Infill blocks stay within 220 of the route, so no lone towers stand on the HQ plain.

Round 9 (2026-11-23): the crater's front was cut down to street level in a wide notch, about 60 degrees each side. A light grey street runs straight into the fractured lip, with a white centre dash and grey concrete kerbs. Beyond each kerb is a reddish paved sidewalk with joints and a yellow line. From the 62% road camera the dark pit walls and the pipe now face the viewer. The plateau top and the overhanging plates are light-to-mid grey asphalt with dark cracked undersides. The plateau body is grey heaved roadbed, and the wrecked block on it was removed. The sewer pipe is mid-grey concrete with darker band rings. It comes out of the right wall on a diagonal, elbows down, and enters a large open bell that faces up and toward the viewer. The HQ gained 60 tier blocks spilling down the upper hull and 4 thin towers, tallest at the centre. Its highway decks are mid grey with white lane dashes.
