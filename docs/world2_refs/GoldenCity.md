# World 2 Track 9 `GoldenCity`: source references

Source: *Frieren: Beyond Journey's End*, Golden Land arc (manga ch. 81-104; anime season 3 adaptation, ep. 38 shot of
the gold region). The Fortress City of Weise, turned to gold by Macht's curse (Diagoldze), sealed under the Great
Barrier of the Golden Land. Reference images are look-only: they sit in `build/world2/refs/GoldenCity/` (gitignored),
are never shipped, and nothing is traced or copied from them. Every prop is rebuilt from primitives in
`tools/blender/world2/biomes/GoldenCity.py` (design doc section 5: no text, numerals or official symbols).
