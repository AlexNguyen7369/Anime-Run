# Character design

Order of identity cues: silhouette (hair, hat, big garments), then colour blocks, then signature marks, then face.

- Check at the real camera distance by shrinking a render, not only in close-ups.
- Model what changes the outline; paint small marks, faces and folds into the texture.
- Hair: few large clumps with clear light and dark planes reads better than many thin shards.
- Keep views consistent: same proportions, colours and marks in front, side, back, three-quarter.
- Turnarounds: neutral pose, orthographic, flat light, no effects or bases.
- Project art direction beats these defaults where they differ (for example a block-hand rule); say so in the validation table.
