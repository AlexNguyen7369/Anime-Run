# IP and canon research

Source order, strongest first: official publisher, studio or Roblox pages; licensed guides; encyclopedic articles citing primary sources; community wikis; fan art (never the sole authority).

- Mark every attribute `supported` (with URL) or `inferred` (seen in an image, no text source).
- A site answering automated tools with 402 or 403 is refusing them: use another source, do not disguise the client.
- Name the version: arc, era, form. Attributes from different eras in one model are a mixed form; report it.
- Licensing: look for a Roblox-supported license or written permission for this exact use. A parody name, credit or no monetization is not a license.
- Record the date of the check, because licensing and designs change.
